# Dawn (v1.0) — Learnable, accessible, translated, robust and launch-ready

[← Roadmap overview](../ROADMAP.md)


**Goal:** Make the full game learnable, accessible, translated and robust on weak phones, then ship to stores. That covers the Gatehouse Drill and contextual Whispers, recorded assists and screen-reader menus, tooltips and a glossary, five languages, captions and spatial cues, the bestiary and achievements, PNG icons and native wrappers with a device lab, a Lite tier with lifecycle resilience, and closure of IP, licence and store readiness.

**Demo moment:** A friend who has never played picks up a phone, learns dodge and sub-weapons from a candle-headed ghost in four minutes, plays in Japanese with captions on, and reaches the Clock Tower on their first run.

**Why now:** The Drill, glossary and translations are written once systems and text are stable after v0.8, so tutorials teach the shipped game and translators don't redo thousands of words. t() and string lint have been enforced since v0.2 (UX-6's core landed early, absorbing STO-8's tables), so extraction is cheap. The Lite tier, Device Lab, wrappers and the IP and store audit all need final content to measure and rate.

**Estimate:** ~12 weeks

**Exit criteria:**

- [ ] The skippable Drill takes ≤ 4 minutes with a single canon mentor, and a Whisper fires on the first encounter with each system and the first unspent stat point.
- [ ] Assists, including the game-speed slider, are recorded in the RunHeader. Menus pass axe-core with zero critical violations, and one-thumb touch layouts plus the layout editor work.
- [ ] Every card, tag, stat, item and door has a tooltip with glossary keywords. The death recap reads BLD-1 attribution, and its 'watch' button opens the TEC-3 replay.
- [ ] Lint finds zero hard-coded strings. Pseudo-locale layout tests pass, and ES, PT-BR, FR, DE and JA ship as lazy chunks outside the initial bundle.
- [ ] Every telegraph cue has a caption and an off-screen indicator, and positional audio is capped at 6 line-of-sight raycasts per frame.
- [ ] The codex covers every monster and boss in tiers, with about 40 achievements and a Hall of Records for the last 100 runs.
- [ ] PNG and maskable icons, safe service-worker updates, and Capacitor (iOS/Android) and Tauri builds all come from CI. The Device Lab passes on ≥ 6 devices (low-end and mid Android, iPhone, iPad, Steam Deck, 4K/120 desktop), and the Lite tier holds 60 FPS on the named weakest phone.
- [ ] The game survives WebGL/WebGPU context loss, phone calls, rotation mid-fight, tab kills and Bluetooth audio latency. The IP audit closes with every canon name rated low risk, a LICENSE and third-party notices exist, the no-IAP/no-ads/no-gacha ADR is recorded, and the store kit and age-rating answers are generated.

#### UX-1 · The Gatehouse Drill — first-run onboarding & contextual Whispers

*UX, onboarding, accessibility & localization · effort L · impact 5/5*

Replace the 15-second keycap strip with a hand-built 3–4 minute training hall run by Sexton Wick, a candle-headed ghost. After that, keep teaching with event-triggered 'Whispers' that fire the first time you meet an Axe Armor windup, a gold-aura elite, or a pile of unspent stat points. **Player value:** New players reach Gorehorn already knowing dash i-frames, telegraph circles, card tags and door routing. Veterans skip it with one button. Every death comes with a lesson.

- **Profile flags & first-run routing** — MetaSave gains persistent 'seen' flags and tutorial state, so the game can tell a new hunter from a veteran and route first launch into setup and the Drill.
  - *Implementation:* game/meta.ts: MetaSave version 2 with flags: Partial<Record<FlagKey, 1>>, using a typed union ('drillDone' | 'drillSkipped' | 'oathDone' | `tip.${TipId}` ...), plus a pure migrateMeta(v1→v2). app.ts boot(): if meta.runs===0 && !flags.drillDone → Oath wizard (Epic 7) → Drill. mainMenu() in screens.ts gains a 'Training Hall' button to replay it.
  - *Acceptance:* Unit test: migrating a v1 save keeps souls, classes, packs, statBonus and history. Playwright: first boot with empty IndexedDB opens the Drill, a reload after finishing opens the title screen, and the flag survives reload.
- **Hand-built room 'The Gatehouse Drill'** — A 40×22 castle hall laid out by hand from an ASCII template: portcullis start, red-carpet lane, archery gallery, bat belfry alcove, an arena with a Cardboard Gorehorn cutout, a chest dais, and two exit doors with Battle and Sanctuary badges.
  - *Implementation:* New src/gen/authored.ts: roomFromTemplate(lines, legend, seed) builds a RoomMap (tiles, carpet, spawn, exits, decor) using existing decor kinds (torch, banner, window, candelabra, pillar, cobweb) and is checked with the existing flood-fill validation. Seed = hashCombine(hashString('gatehouse-drill:v1'), STREAM.tutorial), with STREAM gaining tutorial: 7; streams 1–6 stay as they are. World.loadRoom() takes the result unchanged.
  - *Acceptance:* Gen test: the template is fully connected, spawn clearance ≥ 2 tiles, both exits reachable, and a layout hash snapshot is stable. Existing tests/gen.test.ts snapshots are unchanged.
- **Scripted beats (TutorialDirector)** — Eight beats, each with a goal, a line from Wick and a completion check. (1) Walk 6 tiles along the carpet. (2) Aim and topple 3 Straw Skeletons (contact damage 0). (3) Dash through a 5-arrow volley from a Blunt-Tip Archer (1 dmg). On the first approach the game drops to 0.4× speed for 0.6 s and a ring shows the 0.22 s i-frame window. (4) Hit a 5-bat cluster with the class ability (Shockwave Dash, Singularity Pulse, or Shadow Step's crit). (5) Survive two Cardboard Gorehorn slams (135 radius, 0.85 s telegraph, 0 dmg; 'OOF!' text if caught). (6) Collect souls → forced level-up with a fixed offer (Whetstone, Phase Satellites, Holy Cross), with Auto-assign highlighted. (7) Open a chest. (8) Pick a door after a 5 s map explainer.
  - *Implementation:* src/game/tutorial.ts (headless): Beat[] {id, setup(world), done(world), lineKey}, all built up front. World.update calls an optional director.tick(world) once per tick. Enemies spawn via world.spawnEnemy, then the director overrides E.dmg[i] and hpMul. New WorldEvent {type:'tutorialBeat', id}. app.ts shows Wick's line in a DOM speech bubble. Fail-safes: a beat auto-advances after 3 failed attempts or 45 s, and player HP never drops below 1 in the Drill.
  - *Acceptance:* A headless test drives a scripted intent bot through all 8 beats in order in under 240 s of sim time. The player can't die in the Drill. Pause → 'Skip Drill' ends it within 1 frame and sets drillSkipped. Beat 3 reads CONFIG.dash at runtime, so changing the tuning doesn't break it.
- **Control checklist that ticks off** — Replace the timed hint strip with a checklist that stays up until each action is actually performed. Rows grey out with a tick ('Dash ✓').
  - *Implementation:* Input sets bits in an ActionUsage bitmask (moved, fired, dashed, ability, interacted, opened map). hud.ts hint rows read it, and the rows render through the Epic 3 prompt API. Remove the 15 000 ms setTimeout from app.enterRoom.
  - *Acceptance:* Hints never disappear until all 6 actions are done or 90 s have passed in the room. In Playwright, pressing the bound Dash key marks Dash done within 1 frame. With touch, a swipe-dash ticks the same row.
- **Whispers tip engine** — At least 30 one-shot tips, defined as data and triggered by events. Examples: first Axe Armor windup ('Orange flicker = it is about to charge. Dash sideways!'), first gold-aura elite ('3× HP, 1.4× damage, guaranteed gems'), first Hex Witch hex circle, HP < 30% with a heart on screen, 6+ unspent stat points, first card sharing a tag you own ('[ORBIT] cards now appear 35% more often'), first forge visit, first souls earned.
  - *Implementation:* src/ui/whispers.ts TipEngine consumes new WorldEvents (enemyFirstSeen{def,elite}, windup{def}, lowHp, cardTagMatch). The sim pushes these only on the first occurrence per room, tracked in a Uint8Array per def. A rule table {id, when(evt, ctx), priority} drives display. Limits: at most 1 tip per 20 s, none during boss intros, none while more than 12 enemies are within 300 units. Tips appear as a parchment card with a code-drawn ghost face (SVG in icons.ts) and dismiss after 6 s or on any action. Setting: Tips All / Essential / Off. Seen state is stored in meta.flags.
  - *Acceptance:* A unit test replays a recorded event log and checks tip ids and that tips are ≥ 20 s apart. Each tip shows at most once per profile. Off shows nothing. perf.test 140-enemy tick time stays within ±5% of the current ~0.3 ms.
- **The Ghost's Lesson (on death)** — The game-over screen gives one lesson tied to what killed you, e.g. 'Count Vlador's Blood Spiral took 46% of your HP. It fires in arms; strafe through the gaps.'
  - *Implementation:* endScreen() in screens.ts reads the DamageLog summary from Epic 5 and looks up a lesson table keyed by (srcDef, srcAttack). If nothing matches, it falls back to rotating general tips.
  - *Acceptance:* A test where the player dies to a scripted Gorehorn slam produces the slam lesson key. The same lesson never shows 3 deaths in a row.
- **Unlock features as they become relevant** — Daily Moon, Enter Seed and Unlocks get wax-seal 'NEW!' badges at the point they matter (after run 1, at the first 30 souls). Opening Unlocks the first time shows a two-panel explainer of souls: 0.5 per gem, +10 per floor, +60 on victory.
  - *Implementation:* mainMenu() and unlocksScreen() in screens.ts read meta.flags. Badges are CSS ::after seals. The explainer is a choiceScreen variant whose numbers come from CONFIG.scrapToSouls and meta.soulsFromRun.
  - *Acceptance:* A fresh profile shows no badges. After the first run, Unlocks shows a badge until it is opened once. The explainer's numbers match soulsFromRun() for 5 sample inputs.

**Depends on:** ART: Sexton Wick ghost portrait, Straw Skeleton and Cardboard Gorehorn variants in render/art/characters.ts; AUD: soft whisper chime and music ducking while dialogue is shown; TEC: WorldEvent extensions and an optional director hook in World.update; In-domain: Epic 3 prompt API for all keycaps/glyphs; Epic 5 DamageLog for the Ghost's Lesson; Epic 6 t() so Drill/Whisper lines are written once (land i18n core in sprint 1); Epic 7 Oath wizard before the Drill
**Risks:** Veterans find it tedious → Skip on the first card, plus a 'I've played roguelites' fast path that runs only beats 3, 5 and 6; Scripted beats break when combat tuning changes → beats read CONFIG at runtime and a headless bot test guards them in CI; Tip spam in hectic rooms → density suppression, a 20 s rate limit, and an Essential-only mode; New first-seen events touch the hot loop → Uint8Array first-seen flags per def, pushed at most once per room
**Constraint check:** Visuals reuse existing GraphicsContexts plus DOM/SVG; no assets. The Drill is seeded through the new STREAM.tutorial = 7, so its layout and enemies are reproducible and the existing streams are untouched. The director only writes sim state at tick boundaries using intents, so it stays deterministic. The tip engine runs on events rather than per tick, beat objects are built up front, and World.update stays zero-alloc. Bundle cost is about +8 KB gz. Every line renders through the prompt API, so touch, KB+M and pad (with live switching) all work.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (keep): Unify the mentor with STO-5's Abelard. Build the drill as a GEN-3 prefab, not bespoke room code. First-seen flags as a Uint8Array are fine.
- *player* (keep): Onboarding is a listed gap, and Whispers scale to every new system. One mentor: merge STO-5's Old Abelard with Sexton Wick. The skip path is essential.
- *completeness* (keep): Essential. Sexton Wick duplicates STO-5's Old Abelard mentor; pick one. Drop-off can't be measured without the missing opt-in analytics.

#### UX-4 · Assist Grimoire — motor access, difficulty assists & screen-reader menus

*UX, onboarding, accessibility & localization · effort L · impact 5/5*

Help more people reach the Count. A game-speed slider builds on the existing loop.timeScale. Celeste-style assists are recorded with the run. Every action gets hold vs toggle. Touch gets one-thumb layouts and a drag-and-drop layout editor. Menus move real DOM focus so screen readers can follow. **Player value:** Players with tremor, one usable hand, RSI, low stamina, or who need a screen reader for menus can finish a full 25-minute run. Nobody has to hold LMB for 25 minutes.

- **Game speed 50–100%** — A slider in 5% steps that sets a base multiplier on loop.timeScale. The existing slow-mo effects (death 0.35, boss kill 0.3) multiply on top. Real-time timers (deathT, slowT) stay real-time.
  - *Implementation:* GameLoop gains baseScale. In app.ts, every 'this.loop.timeScale = 1' (enterRoom, resume, slowT expiry) becomes '= this.speed', and the slow-mo sites multiply by it. The sim dt stays 1/120.
  - *Acceptance:* Sim test: the same intent stream at 0.5 and at 1.0 gives an identical World state hash after 12 000 ticks. Playwright: at 0.6 the Gorehorn fight takes 1/0.6 of the wall-clock time, ±5%.
- **Assist mode (recorded, never hidden)** — Options: damage taken 25/50/75/100%; 'Sanctified' (HP never drops below 1); 'Second Wind' (once per floor, revive at 50% HP with 2 s of i-frames); post-hit i-frames +0.3 s (0.55 → 0.85); enemy projectile speed 60–100%. Assisted runs show a lily 'Assisted' badge on the end screen, in seed history and on the Daily Moon card. There is no soul penalty.
  - *Implementation:* New game/assist.ts: AssistConfig packed into an assistMask uint32 plus value bytes and passed to World. hurtPlayer multiplies damage before rounding; the death branch checks Second Wind; enemyAi multiplies shotSpeed. RunSave and SeedRecord gain an assist field, latched if any assist was ever on during the run. Generation code never reads it.
  - *Acceptance:* Floor/room generation hashes are identical with and without assists. At 50%, a raw 26 hit against 0 armor deals 13. Second Wind fires once per floor. The end screen badge shows exactly when the latch is set.
- **Hold vs toggle for every action** — Fire: Hold / Toggle / Always. Dash: Tap / Hold-to-repeat when off cooldown. Ability: Tap / Hold-to-aim, release-to-cast (Code Weaver places Singularity Pulse precisely with a ground reticle). A dedicated auto-fire toggle (CapsLock / L3 by default).
  - *Implementation:* Input keeps the toggle and latch state on the input side and writes Intent.fire, dash and ability from it. Hold-to-aim sets Intent.abilityAiming, which only the renderer reads (for the reticle), and emits the ability edge on release. Toggle state resets when the input method changes, so fire can't get stuck on.
  - *Acceptance:* KB+M with Fire = Toggle: one LMB click fires continuously until the next click (intent sampling test). On pad, hold-to-aim shows the reticle and casts at the stick direction on release. Recorded intents replay to the same state hash.
- **One-handed touch layouts & layout editor** — 'Thumb Duo' (the current layout), plus 'Solo Left' and 'Solo Right': one floating stick anywhere on the chosen half, auto-aim and auto-fire forced on, ability and dash as 72 px buttons on an arc around the thumb's resting spot, and double-tap to dash. A 'Fixed stick' option. A drag-to-place editor for ability, dash, interact and pause, with sizes 60–160%.
  - *Implementation:* Input.onPointerDown's zone logic gains a layout mode. Hud.layoutTouch reads normalized, safe-area-aware positions from settings instead of fixed calc() strings. The editor is a DOM overlay screen with pointer dragging, an 8 px snap grid, and no-go zones around the minimap and gear.
  - *Acceptance:* Playwright with a single touch pointer finishes the Drill in Solo Right. With iPhone notch emulation, buttons never overlap safe-area insets. Edited positions persist across reload.
- **Fewer decisions mid-fight** — Auto-allocate stats using the existing statPanel auto() class preferences. A 'Suggested' ribbon on the level-up card that matches the most owned tags. Auto-open chests on touch. 'Show level-up: Immediately / After room clear', so the modal doesn't interrupt fights.
  - *Implementation:* app.ts openLevelUp and openStats branch on settings. The suggestion comes from the same weights as offerCards (CONFIG.cardTagBonus). A deferred level-up waits for the 'cleared' WorldEvent.
  - *Acceptance:* With Auto-allocate on, the stats modal never opens and points follow the class order. With 'After room clear', no modal opens before the cleared event (sim + app test).
- **Screen-reader-ready menus** — Nav moves real DOM focus. Screens become labelled dialogs. Toasts and Whispers go to a polite live region. Toggles expose aria-pressed and labels are tied to their controls. A 'status' hotkey reads out the HUD: 'HP 180 of 240, Level 7, 312 gems, Floor 2, Battle 3 of 6'.
  - *Implementation:* nav.ts focus() calls el.focus({preventScroll: true}). UI.open sets role=dialog, aria-modal and aria-labelledby on the screen's h2. hud.ts toasts render into an aria-live region, rate-limited to 1 announcement per 2 s. settingsPanel generates ids for label/for. The status action comes from Epic 3's action map.
  - *Acceptance:* axe-core in Playwright reports 0 serious or critical violations on every screen. A manual NVDA and VoiceOver script completes New Run → class select → level-up pick → settings change without looking at the screen.

**Depends on:** TEC: Playwright + axe-core in CI; a sim state-hash helper; GAMEPLAY: agreement that assists never touch generation; a projectile-speed hook in enemyAi.ts; META/achievements: decide whether assisted runs count (recommend yes, flagged); AUD: game speed leaves music real-time unless the audio domain adds tempo follow; In-domain: Epic 3 action map for the toggle/status bindings; Epic 7 schema and presets
**Risks:** Assists may be seen as cheating in Daily Moon comparisons → a separate visible 'Assisted' bracket and badge, never hidden; Screen-reader live regions can spam → throttling, and HUD announcements are opt-in; Solo layouts can collide with the minimap and gear button → no-go zones in the editor; Toggle fire left on during menus → toggle state clears when a menu opens or the input method changes
**Constraint check:** No assets. Game speed never changes dt. Assist settings live in a packed config saved with the run and never read by gen/*, so runs stay reproducible from runSeed + floorIndex + attemptCounter, with assistMask as a recorded combat parameter. Toggles are input-layer state that produce the same Intent stream replays already record. The hot loop gains one multiply and one branch in hurtPlayer, with no allocation. Bundle cost about +5 KB gz. Works across touch, KB+M and pad with live switching.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (keep): Game speed through loop.timeScale is determinism-safe, but the projectile-speed assist must be recorded in RunRules. Screen-reader menus are realistic since the UI is already DOM.
- *player* (keep): Assists widen the audience at low cost, and recorded assists keep dailies honest. Absorb BAL-7's assist options.
- *completeness* (keep): Strong. Assist flags must flow into replays, boards and dailies. The game-speed slider interacts with hit-stop, dodge windows and audio tempo; test these together.

#### UX-5 · Codex of Whispers — tooltips, keyword glossary, damage log & death recap

*UX, onboarding, accessibility & localization · effort M · impact 4/5*

Every highlighted word becomes something you can look up. Hover, focus or long-press any card, tag, stat, item or door to get a parchment tooltip, with keyword definitions inside. Every hit is recorded in a fixed-size, zero-alloc ring buffer that feeds a 'Your Final Moments' recap and a DPS breakdown. **Player value:** Players learn what [ORBIT] does, can compare a Grim Sickle against their Lv3 Raven Fang before equipping, can re-read owned cards mid-run, and know exactly what killed them.

- **Keyword glossary & markup** — About 30 entries. The tags ARCANE/KINETIC/CRIT/ORBIT/HOLY/BLOOD/FLAME, each noting '+35% offer weight per owned tag'. Burn (5 dmg/s per stack, 3 s). Chain (20%, 3 foes, 60%). Familiar. Elite (3× HP, 1.4× damage). Telegraph. I-frames (dash 0.22 s, after hit 0.55 s). Armor (100 armor halves damage). Energy. Cooldown cap 60%. Crit cap 75%. Gems vs Souls. Forge (+20% per level).
  - *Implementation:* game/glossary.ts holds the data, with icon names, and pulls its numbers from CONFIG. Card and event text gains [[kw]] tokens. dom.ts richText() outputs <span class='kw' data-kw>. Tag chips get data-kw too.
  - *Acceptance:* A test checks that every [[kw]] in CARDS and EVENTS resolves to a glossary entry, and that glossary numbers match CONFIG (e.g. caps.cdr = 0.6).
- **Tooltip system** — A single reused tooltip. Opens on mouse hover (300 ms), on a 'Details' action from keyboard or pad (Y / △ / X-top / Tab) or after a 500 ms focus dwell, and on touch long-press (350 ms) with a haptic tick. Placement avoids the screen edges. One nested level: press Details again on a keyword inside a card's tooltip.
  - *Implementation:* ui/tooltip.ts creates one element that is reused for every hover, so no DOM is created per hover. Nav gains a focus-change hook. Content comes from data-tip and data-kw providers.
  - *Acceptance:* Playwright: the tooltip appears within 350 ms for hover, focus and long-press. It never clips at 844×390. Esc / B closes the nested level first.
- **Full card view & tag ledger mid-run** — Pause → Cards shows full descriptions and stack counts (today it shows only icon and name), plus a ledger: 'ORBIT ×3 → next ORBIT card +105% likely · HOLY ×2 → +70%'.
  - *Implementation:* The cards tab of pauseScreen in screens.ts uses a compact cardEl variant. The ledger is computed from player.cards × CONFIG.cardTagBonus.
  - *Acceptance:* Snapshot test: owning Phase Satellites and Bat Familiar shows 'ORBIT ×2 (+70%)'. Every owned card's description is visible in the tab.
- **Item comparison & stat explanations** — The item-found and shop screens show ▲/▼ differences against the equipped item at its forge level, e.g. 'Grim Sickle DMG 11 ▲ +3.0 vs Raven Fang Lv3' and 'Atk speed −9.6% ▼', plus the inherited forge level after a swap (ceil(old/2)). Character sheet rows explain formulas and caps: 'Armor 18 → hits ×0.85', 'CDR 22% / 60%'.
  - *Implementation:* A pure compareItems(a, lvA, b, lvB) in equipment.ts using itemStats and equipScale. sheet() rows gain data-tip.
  - *Acceptance:* compareItems unit test on 6 pairs. The inherited-level text matches app.offerItem's math for levels 1–6.
- **Damage log ring buffer** — Records every hit taken and dealt, with its source: enemy type and attack. Examples: contact, Bone Archer arrow, Gorehorn slam/charge/rockfall, Rune Colossus spikes/spiral, Count Vlador hellfire/blood spiral/bat swarm on one side; burn, chain, familiars, Holy Cross, Axe, Requiem on the other.
  - *Implementation:* game/damageLog.ts: a 256-entry structure-of-arrays ring (Uint32 tick, Uint8 srcDef, Uint8 srcAttack, Float32 raw, Float32 final, Float32 hpAfter) plus Float32Array per-source totals. ProjectileStore and HazardStore gain Uint8Array srcDef/srcAttack fields, set at spawn. hurtPlayer and hitEnemy take an attack-id argument (default 0).
  - *Acceptance:* perf.test: the 140-enemy tick stays ≤ 0.35 ms and allocates 0. A hazard spawned by Gorehorn's slam is logged with srcDef = ogre and srcAttack = slam.
- **'Your Final Moments' recap & DPS breakdown** — The end screen gets a panel with: the last 8 hits, each with a code-drawn enemy portrait; the killing blow highlighted ('Slain by Count Vlador's Blood Spiral — 38 dmg, armor blocked 12'); damage taken by source as bars; and outgoing damage by source ('Twin Daggers 58% · Holy Cross 21% · Burn 9% · Familiars 12%'). The Training Hall gets a live DPS meter.
  - *Implementation:* endScreen in screens.ts gains a recap section with SVG bars. Enemy portraits come from a portrait() variant extracted per enemy type and cached.
  - *Acceptance:* When a scripted Bone Archer arrow kills the player, the killing blow reads 'Bone Archer — Arrow'. Source percentages sum to 100 ±1.
- **Door & node previews** — Near an exit door, a pill shows where it leads: 'Elite — 3× HP foes, rare chest · 2 rooms to Boss'. The map tab gets per-node tooltips.
  - *Implementation:* hud.ts reads world.room.exits targets and computes distance to the boss over run.graph (reachableFrom in gen/graph.ts). mapView nodes get data-tip.
  - *Acceptance:* Across a 50-seed sweep, standing within 2 tiles of any door always shows the correct node type and distance to the boss.

**Depends on:** GAMEPLAY/COMBAT: attack-id plumbing in enemyAi.ts and playerCombat.ts (those files' owners); ART: small enemy portrait poses for the recap; META: share glossary data with any bestiary/codex built in another domain; In-domain: Epic 6 t() for glossary strings; Epic 3 'Details' action; Epic 1 Ghost's Lesson reads the log
**Risks:** Attribution plumbing touches hot code → typed arrays only, with a perf-test gate in CI; Too many tooltips on touch → long-press only, never on tap; Glossary numbers drifting from tuning → always computed from CONFIG
**Constraint check:** DOM and SVG only. The damage log is fixed-size typed arrays the sim writes but never reads back into decisions, so the sim stays zero-alloc and deterministic. Bundle cost about +5 KB gz. Works with mouse hover, touch long-press, and the pad/keyboard Details action.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (revise): Keep the glossary, tooltips, item comparisons and door previews. The damage-log ring buffer duplicates BLD-1 attribution and MET-1's tally; the death recap lives in MET-6.
- *player* (keep): Tooltips and a glossary become essential once cards, statuses and relics multiply. The damage log must reuse BLD-1's ledger; one death recap shared with MET-6.
- *completeness* (revise): Tooltips and glossary are great. The damage log and death recap duplicate MET-6, BAL-8, BLD-1 and SOC-4; consume BLD-1's attribution instead.

#### UX-6 · Tongues of the Keep — i18n framework & first languages

*UX, onboarding, accessibility & localization · effort XL · impact 4/5*

Move about 650 hardcoded strings (roughly 6,500 words) out of screens.ts, hud.ts, app.ts and the data tables into typed catalogs. Add simple plural/select handling and Intl number formatting, make fonts and outlined text work per script, and ship Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German and Japanese, plus a pseudo-locale for testing. **Player value:** Most mobile players don't play in English. The cartoon-gothic tone (the merchant's 'Heh heh... what're ya buyin'?') should land in their own language.

- **i18n core** — t(key, params) with typed keys and plural/select support. Intl.NumberFormat compact notation replaces formatNum's hardcoded 'k'/'m' (e.g. '7,1 k' in French). Plurals use Intl.PluralRules. Formatter instances are cached.
  - *Implementation:* src/i18n/index.ts (about 2 KB gz). src/i18n/locales/en.ts is declared 'as const' and defines the MessageKey type. Other locales load via dynamic import() as separate chunks. vite.config's service-worker generator already precaches every bundle file, so they work offline. A missing key falls back to English with a dev-only warning.
  - *Acceptance:* tsc fails on an unknown key. Plural tests cover en, fr, ja and ru rules. t() without params returns an interned string and allocates nothing.

#### AUD-4 · Hear Everywhere, See Every Sound: spatial audio and audio accessibility

*Music & sound · effort M · impact 4/5*

- **Camera listener and 2D pan law** — The listener is renderer.camX/camY. Pan is equal-power over dx divided by the half view width, clamped to ±0.85.   - *Implementation:* src/audio/spatial.ts pure function gainPanLp(dx, dy, halfW, prio, out). RenderFx.listenerX is replaced by listener {x, y, halfW} set in App.render. Boss roars in enemyAi.ts now pass x/y.

#### GAP-8 · TEC-9 Every Phone Survives: Lite tier, global budget ledger and app-lifecycle and GPU resilience

*Gaps found in review · effort L · impact 5/5*

Name the weakest phones Crimson Keep must run on and add a Lite preset for them. Own the combined cost of every epic in one CI-enforced budget ledger, and measure battery and thermal drain at each milestone. Make the app survive what phones actually do: WebGL or WebGPU context loss, calls and audio interruptions, rotation mid-fight, tab kills, and Bluetooth audio latency. **Player value:** The game runs on a budget Android at a stable 30 or 60 FPS without overheating the phone. It recovers from a GPU context loss instead of crashing, resumes mid-fight after the OS kills the tab, and timing still feels right with Bluetooth earbuds.

- **Lite preset and named reference devices** — Reference devices: Mali-G52 MC2 (Galaxy A12 or Redmi 9 class) and Adreno 610 (Moto G9 Play class), both with 3–4 GB RAM, plus iPhone 8 or SE 2 as the iOS floor. Lite has a selectable 30 or 60 FPS cap, render scale 0.5, DPR capped at 1.25, 400 particles, and no bloom, glow, lighting (ART-5), weather (ART-7), decals or rig secondary motion (ART-3). It uses GOV-2's crowd LOD and coalesced damage numbers.
  - *Implementation:* config.ts adds PRESETS.lite and a PRESET_ORDER entry. PresetSpec gains lighting, weather, decals, rigDetail and actorLod fields, which every ART epic must honour. device.ts classifyGpu maps Mali-G5x and Adreno 60x/61x to Lite, and settings.ts exposes the 30/60 choice.
  - *Acceptance:* On the Mali-G52 reference device, Lite-30 holds a p95 frame time of at most 34 ms in the heaviest combat room, and Lite-60 averages at least 55 FPS in standard rooms. The sim state hash matches Ultra (GOV-2). The type system forces every new ART preset flag to define a Lite value.
- **Sim catch-up and graceful slow-down** — Today loop.ts runs up to 30 catch-up steps and then silently zeroes the accumulator. Replace this with maxStepsPerFrame derived from the frame cap (for example 8 at 30 FPS). When a frame exceeds it, time dilates gracefully, the dropped sim time is reported to the perf monitor, and the dev overlay shows it. App timers (deathT, slowT) move to sim time through GOV-1's timeCtl.
  - *Implementation:* core/loop.ts gets maxStepsPerFrame and a droppedSimTime counter. render/perf.ts consumes the dilation, and the app.ts timers read World.timeCtl.
  - *Acceptance:* Fake-clock unit tests: at a 30 FPS cap, a 100 ms hitch runs 8 steps, records about 33 ms of dilation, and doesn't spiral. A steady 30 FPS runs 4 steps per frame. Resuming a hidden tab never runs more than one frame's worth of steps.
- **Global performance budget ledger in CI** — One ledger, with a row per epic and platform totals, covering: sim time per tick (Node heavy scenarios times a measured mobile factor), draw calls and GPU memory (Playwright with Pixi stats), resident GraphicsContexts, render-texture MB, gzipped bundle KB, IndexedDB MB, audio voices and HUD DOM nodes.   - *Implementation:* perf/budget.json holds the ledger. scripts/budget.ts aggregates vitest perf output, Rollup bundle stats and a Playwright ?bench=heavy scenario. tests/perf.test.ts gains two scenarios: 160 enemies, 256 hazards, 600 projectiles and every card; and a boss phase with summons.
  - *Acceptance:* Every epic that touches runtime has a ledger row, and the CI artifact shows actuals against budget. A synthetic PR that adds 200 KB gzipped fails. The mobile factor is recalibrated on the reference phones at every milestone.
- **Battery and thermal drain per milestone** — A 20-minute scripted run, with a TEC-7 bot driving the Intent, on each reference phone at Medium and at Lite.   - *Implementation:* A ?bench=soak mode runs on the bot driver. Results are logged locally and exported through TRU-1's diagnostics, and the procedure lives in docs/perf/soak.md.
  - *Acceptance:* A soak table is published at every PRD-1 gate. A battery regression of more than 20% against the previous milestone blocks the gate.
- **GPU context loss and restore** — Handle webglcontextlost and webglcontextrestored, plus WebGPU's device.lost. On loss the game pauses. A GpuResource registry then rebuilds everything Pixi doesn't restore itself: baked atlases (TEC-5, ART-4), light, decal and vista render textures (ART-5, ART-7), and the bloom filter.
