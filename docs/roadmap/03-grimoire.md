# Grimoire (v0.4) — Data-driven combat, generation, readability and sound

[← Roadmap overview](../ROADMAP.md)


**Goal:** Replace the hard-wired enemy switch, damage path, floor themes and SFX table with registries that every content epic will write into. Make each threat readable and fair in any palette on any device, give every sound a mixer, and ship full remapping before new actions arrive.

**Demo moment:** In deuteranopia mode a Bone Archer winds up just off-screen. An edge chevron and a bow creak warn you, and the charge lane reads by its shape, not its colour. Then a brand-new monster asks a question the original seven never did.

**Why now:** ENM-1, BLD-1, BIO-1 and GEN-1 are the four substrates that every enemy, card, biome and trap epic writes into, so content built before them would be throwaway. ART-2 (absorbing UX-2) ships with ENM-1 so there is exactly one telegraph grammar, and BAL-1 consumes ENM-1's AttackIds. The mixer and contract tests must exist before ~95 sounds and adaptive stems arrive. UX-3 builds on M1's Intent v2 and must precede BLD-3's sub-weapon action. ART-8 is the dev shell ART-3 needs for authoring rigs.

**Estimate:** ~13 weeks

**Exit criteria:**

- [ ] Brains plus WINDUP/ACTIVE/RECOVER move sets replace enemyAi.ts's switch. The original 7 enemies and 3 bosses are ported with golden-hash parity, and 3 proof monsters each take at most 80 lines of data plus one draw function.
- [ ] The typed damage pipeline (damage types, resistances, proc coefficients, effect registry) is live, its attribution source table feeds the death screen, and hitEnemy no longer allocates a string per hit.
- [ ] The biome registry reproduces byte-identical rooms for 10,000 epoch-1 seeds. The epoch-2 pipeline (per-pass streams, setTile, stable entity ids, RoomDelta) is merged dark behind a flag.
- [ ] Every AttackId has a telegraph at or above the minimum and an audio tell. No enemy fires from outside a fixed sim-side logical rect centred on the player, wave spawns respect a minimum distance from the player, and a full-HP one-shot floor (Death's Door) applies.
- [ ] The 5-shape telegraph grammar and semantic palette ship. Deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia and high-contrast modes are token swaps with contrast tests in CI. The FlashGovernor caps flashing at ≤ 3 Hz and no text renders below 12 px.
- [ ] The mixer has buses, priority voice stealing, ducking and snapshots. About 45 SfxDefs with seeded variants exist, a 140-enemy swarm stays inside the voice budget, and DSP golden-hash and sim-to-sound contract tests run in Node.
- [ ] Full remapping works on keyboard and gamepad, with layout-aware key labels and code-drawn Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo and Steam Deck glyphs. ?artlab=1 works and its chunk is excluded from the build precache.

#### ENM-1 · Grimoire of Behaviours: data-driven enemy AI toolkit

*Enemies, elites & bosses · effort L · impact 5/5*

Replace enemyAi.ts's switch with a composable kit: brains dispatched by a numeric id, declarative move sets with WINDUP → ACTIVE → RECOVER phases, and libraries for telegraph shapes and bullet patterns. Traits drive hit reactions, a poise meter enables staggers, and enemies gain awareness. After this, a new monster is about 60 lines of data plus one draw function. **Player value:** Every monster telegraphs before it hurts you, has 2–3 moves with readable tells, and can be outplayed. Fights feel fair, varied and very Castlevania. Almost every later epic depends on this one.

- **Brain registry and extended EnemyStore** — Split src/game/enemyAi.ts into src/game/ai/ and dispatch each enemy through a numeric brain id, removing every string compare. EnemyDef gains: brain, moves, traits, material, height, kbResist, poise, tier ('minor', 'elite', 'mini' or 'boss'), parts and hidden. EnemyStore gains these columns: brain, move, movePhase, phaseT, cd (4 strided cooldown slots), affix (Uint16), parent (Int16), aware, z (hop height), slowT/slowMul, buffT, poiseCur, token, memX/memY and aux2.
  - *Implementation:* Create src/game/ai/{index,steering}.ts and src/game/ai/brains/{chase,flutter,ranged,charger,gorehorn,colossus,vlador}.ts. First port each existing case verbatim as a behaviour-preserving refactor. Register the brains in `BRAIN_FNS: BrainFn[]`, where BrainFn is (w, i, dt) => void and writes the desired velocity to w.out. updateEnemies keeps the shared steering, separation, slide and contact code. Add the new columns in src/game/stores.ts at POOLS.enemies size (about 64 B × 512 ≈ 32 KB) and zero them in world.spawnEnemy. Widen EnemyStore.def to Uint16. The renderer's ENEMY_HEIGHT table moves into EnemyDef.height.
  - *Acceptance:* All existing vitest suites pass. A new golden replay test (Run(1234), 3 rooms, scripted intent) yields the same quantized state hash before and after the port. The perf.test average tick stays ≤ 1.1× the pre-refactor baseline. A grep for `d.key ===` in src/game returns zero matches.
- **MoveSet state machine with readability rules** — Each enemy gets a declarative move list. A move has: id, weight, range [min, max], cooldown, windup, active, recover, telegraph spec, needsLos, token class, action and params. A shared runner picks an eligible move with seeded weights, then steps it through WINDUP, ACTIVE and RECOVER, spawning the telegraph at windup start. Readability rules: (1) any move dealing ≥ 15% of a level-1 hero's max HP (about 18 damage against Vanguard's 120) needs a windup ≥ 0.45 s; (2) a lane or cone needs windup ≥ (halfWidth + 14 px) / 200 px/s + 0.3 s, so it can be walked out of at base Vanguard speed without dashing.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/game/ai/moves.ts with runMoves(w, i, dt, set). It selects with w.rng.weightedIndex over a preallocated Float32Array scratch buffer, so nothing allocates. Add src/game/ai/actions.ts with a numeric enum of actions: LUNGE, THRUST, SLAM, FAN, RADIAL, SPIRAL, LOB, BEAM, SUMMON, TELEPORT, BLINK_BEHIND, ROAR_BUFF, SPAWN_HAZARD, PULL. Pack params into one Float32Array per def at module load. validateDefs() throws on any rule violation; it runs in dev builds and in tests. Port Axe Armor's charger onto the runner as the reference implementation.
  - *Acceptance:* Over 10,000 ticks, an enemy with 2 moves never starts a move whose cooldown slot is > 0. validateDefs() rejects a test def with a 0.2 s windup on a 30-damage lane. The ported Axe Armor reproduces the legacy charger's per-tick (state, t) trace for 600 ticks.
- **Telegraph shape library** — Add new hazard kinds:
- LINE: a rotated lane with angle, length and width.
- CONE: angle, arc and radius.
- DONUT: inner and outer radius, with a safe centre.
- FOLLOW: a circle that tracks the player, then locks 0.3 s before detonating.
- ENEMY_FIRE: persistent damaging ground that ticks every 0.4 s.
- BEAM: a LINE that deals damage while active.
- ROTOR: a LINE that rotates around its owner.

One colour language covers every enemy: an orange fill grows toward detonation, red means active damage, violet means pull, cyan means slow, and a white edge pops 0.1 s before impact.
  - *Implementation:* In src/game/stores.ts, HazardStore gains these columns: ang, len, arc, inner, owner (Int16), spin and team. Raise POOLS.hazards from 64 to 256. In world.updateHazards, add point-in-OBB, sector and annulus tests that project the player into hazard space using cos/sin cached per hazard per tick. A ROTOR follows its owner through the parent column. In renderer.ts, build one shared unit GraphicsContext per shape (lane rect, 90° sector, a donut drawn as two arcs) and scale it, just as hazardCtx does today. A 'High-contrast telegraphs' setting doubles outline width and adds hatch stripes.
  - *Acceptance:* For every shape, collision unit tests sample points 1 px inside and outside each edge at rotations of 0°, 45°, 90° and 173°. A ROTOR at 40°/s completes 360° within ±1°. In the perf test, 256 live hazards plus 150 enemies keep the Node tick under 1.0 ms. A headless-Chromium screenshot test renders every telegraph kind at render scale 0.5 and at 1.0.
- **Bullet-pattern library and projectile motion flags** — Widen ProjectileStore.flags to Uint16 and add these flags:
- PF_SINE: weave with amplitude and frequency.
- PF_ACCEL: accelerate.
- PF_CURVE: angular velocity.
- PF_DELAY: hang for N s, then aim at the player.
- PF_SPLIT: burst into N bullets on expiry.
- PF_BOUNCE: reflect off walls n times.
- PF_ORBIT_OWNER: circle a parent enemy.
- PF_CANCELABLE: Arc Slash, the Holy Cross and the Axe destroy it.

Add src/game/ai/patterns.ts with these patterns: radial, fan, spiral, ringWithGap, laneWall, burst, flower and bulletText. bulletText uses a 5×5 bitmap font to spell words in bullets.
  - *Implementation:* Add pa, pb and owner columns to ProjectileStore. In world.updateProjectiles, branch per flag with closed-form maths and no closures, and add an enemy-team PF_RETURN that flies back to the owner. Patterns are plain functions (w, x, y, kind, params) that loop over w.enemyShot. CONFIG.enemyBulletCap = 900 is the same on every device, so results stay deterministic; once the cap is hit, the oldest enemy bullet is recycled. Raise POOLS.projectiles from 1024 to 2048. Add new enemy PROJ kinds (FEATHER, GLYPH, ICE, SICKLE, PETAL, EMBER, SPEAR, SNAKE, SCARAB) with Pen draw functions in src/render/art/props.ts and PROJ_GLOW tints.
  - *Acceptance:* A snapshot test records bullet positions for every pattern and flag after 120 ticks with a fixed seed. ringWithGap always leaves a gap ≥ 2 × player radius + 12 px at 200 px from its source. In the perf test, 1,500 live enemy bullets keep the Node tick under 1.2 ms. A PF_CANCELABLE fireball hit by Arc Slash is released and drops one soul spark.
- **Traits, materials, poise and stagger** — Make hit reactions data-driven.
- Material: one of bone, flesh, stone, ecto, metal, paper or cloth. It selects hit and death particles and the SFX.
- Traits: a bitmask of UNDEAD, BEAST, DEMON, CONSTRUCT, SPIRIT, AQUATIC, FLYING and SHIELDED_FRONT. Card hooks can read it, e.g. HOLY ×1.25 against UNDEAD.
- Poise: a meter on large monsters (Axe Armor 60, Mummy 80, mini-bosses 220, bosses 450). When it breaks, the enemy shows 'STAGGER!', is stunned for 1.2 s and takes +25% damage. Bosses are stunned for 2.0 s and then have a 25 s lockout before they can stagger again.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/game/ai/materials.ts with a MATERIAL_FX table that replaces the `bony` checks in world.hitEnemy and killEnemy. kbResist replaces the hard-coded 0.08 (boss) and 0.5 (elite) knockback scales. Poise damage = hit damage × 1.5 on a crit × a class multiplier set in CONFIG (Vanguard 1.4, Weaver 0.8, Phantom 1.0). Add a 'bossStagger' WorldEvent for the HUD.
  - *Acceptance:* src/game/world.ts and src/render/renderer.ts contain no d.key string compares. In a scripted 30 s damage test, a 450-poise boss staggers exactly once and never twice within 25 s. A recording FxSink sees 'debris' on stone hits and 'spark' on metal hits.
- **Awareness, dormancy and group alert** — Enemies move through three states:
- DORMANT: e.g. a Mummy in a sarcophagus, a Gargoyle posing as a statue, a roosting bat or a perched raven.
- IDLE: wanders within 3 tiles of its anchor.
- ALERT: hunts the player.

An enemy wakes when:
- it has line of sight to the player within `sight` (default 360 px),
- it takes damage,
- the player makes noise (dash 220 px, ability 320 px, shrine use),
- an ally alerts it. Alerts chain through the spatial hash with a 0.2 s delay per link.

A cartoon '!' pops above an enemy when it wakes. Enemies from wave spawns always arrive ALERT. If combat has run for 20 s, every enemy is forced ALERT, so room clear can never soft-lock.
  - *Implementation:* Use the aware column plus memX/memY: after losing line of sight, ranged enemies search the last-seen point for 3 s. Noise is a per-tick ring buffer on World (Float32Array(32) storing x, y, r). Dormant anchors come from room.decor entries (coffin, pillar, banner, window, candelabra) through a new SpawnEntry.anchor field set in planSpawns.
  - *Acceptance:* A dormant Mummy stays still for 60 s while the player keeps > 400 px away without line of sight, and wakes on the same tick it takes damage. A 4-enemy cluster is fully ALERT within 1.0 s of one member seeing the player. Every enemy is ALERT by 20 s of combat.
- **Sim/FX RNG split and AI test harness** — Move cosmetic rolls inside the sim (burn embers, charge smoke, hit jitter) to w.fxRng, so presentation tweaks can never perturb gameplay. Add a harness, runEncounter(defs, seed, ticks, bot), that returns a quantized state hash. Use it for:
- golden hashes per enemy and per boss,
- a move-coverage assertion,
- a 'never stuck' check.
  - *Implementation:* In world.ts, add `fxRng = new Rng(hashCombine(room.seed, 0xf1f1))`. Add tests/ai.test.ts and tests/helpers/bots.ts. The scripted dodger bot samples 16 directions and scores each by proximity to projectiles and hazards, and a recording FxSink captures effects. The hash is FNV over Math.round(x × 4) and Math.round(hp) every 60 ticks.
  - *Acceptance:* Doubling burn-ember particle counts leaves every golden hash unchanged. Every boss move id activates at least once in 120 s of bot play. No ALERT enemy stays within 64 px of one spot for 10 s while the player is reachable.

**Depends on:** needs TEC CI pipeline so golden-hash and move-coverage tests gate every PR; needs ART: renderer support for the new hazard shapes (shared unit GraphicsContexts) and the '!' alert pop; needs UI/settings toggle for high-contrast telegraphs; CARDS domain may consume traits (HOLY vs UNDEAD) and PF_CANCELABLE (parry-style cards); PLAYER domain: slow, pull and stun fields for enemy effects

#### BLD-1 · E1 · The Hit Ledger: Damage Pipeline 2.0

*Combat systems & buildcraft · effort L · impact 5/5*

Rebuild the damage path as a staged, typed, data-driven pipeline with damage types, resistances, proc coefficients, an open effect registry and a separate combat PRNG stream. Every later combat epic builds on it. **Player value:** Builds behave the way their cards say. Holy smites skeletons, gargoyles shrug off swords, crits feel huge, every source shows its own coloured numbers, and the pause screen shows what is doing the damage ('Holy Cross 31% · Arc Slash 44% · Burn 12%').

- **Hit record + dealHit() pipeline** — Replace the monolithic hitEnemy with fixed stages: base → increased% (additive) → more× (multiplicative) → crit → resist and penetration → vulnerability → variance → apply → triggers. Every damage source goes through it: slash, orbs, daggers, shards, cross, axe, holy fire, wells, shockwave, familiars, scythe, chain, requiem, thorns and DoTs.
  - *Implementation:* New src/game/damage.ts exports dealHit(w, hit) and a preallocated stack of 4 Hit records for nested procs, so nothing is allocated per hit. Hit fields: target, amount, type (Uint8 DMG), src (Uint8 SRC), flags (CAN_CRIT|FORCE_CRIT|NO_CRIT|NO_PROC|DOT|AOE|HEAVY), procCoef, kx/ky/kb, depth. World.hitEnemy stays as a thin adapter that maps critMode to flags, so call sites in playerCombat.ts, world.ts (projectiles, hazards, chainFrom, thorns) and enemyAi.ts (burn) can migrate one at a time. The tuning constants (variance 0.92–1.08, knockback scale 0.08 boss / 0.5 elite) move to CONFIG.combat.
  - *Acceptance:* (1) Parity: with no new mods, 10,000 seeded hits through dealHit match the legacy formula exactly, in both damage values and RNG draw counts. (2) Stage golden test: 100 base, +50% FIRE, 50% FIRE resist and ×1.6 crit gives 120 before variance. (3) The tests/perf.test.ts heavy tick stays under 4 ms and within 10% of the pre-refactor baseline. (4) An ESLint no-restricted-syntax rule bans object/array literals and closures inside damage.ts.
- **Eight damage types + typed scaling** — DMG = PHYS, HOLY, FIRE, FROST, SHOCK, POISON, BLOOD, ARCANE. Each source declares one: Arc Slash and daggers PHYS; Homing Orb and Singularity ARCANE; Cross, Holy Water and Requiem HOLY; Burning Hex FIRE; Storm Chain SHOCK; the Moonlit Dash trail FROST ('moonfrost'); Reaper's Scythe BLOOD. Cards, items and relics supply typed % mods and conversion mods, so a 'Hallowed' blade can turn 40% of its PHYS into HOLY.
  - *Implementation:* In stats.ts, add these MOD_KEYS: physPct, holyPct, firePct, frostPct, shockPct, poisonPct, bloodPct, arcanePct, convHoly, convFire, resistPen, statusChance, statusPower, statusDur. DerivedStats gains typeMul: Float32Array(8), allocated once and refilled on recompute. theme.ts gets DMG_COLORS, which fx.text uses for damage numbers. When a hit is partly converted, its resist is weighted by the type split.
  - *Acceptance:* A test iterates SOURCES and asserts each has a valid type. +25% holyPct raises Holy Cross damage by exactly 25% and leaves Arc Slash unchanged. 50% conversion applies weighted resist correctly. Numbers render in type colour: PHYS white, HOLY #fff0a8, FIRE #ff8a2a, FROST #9fe0ff, SHOCK #ffe14d, POISON #8dff5a, BLOOD #ff4d6a, ARCANE #c58cff.
- **Enemy materials & resistance tables** — Each enemy gets a material (flesh, bone, stone, spirit or iron) and a resist vector:
- Skeleton and Bone Archer: HOLY −50%, immune to poison and bleed.
- Ghoul: FIRE −30%, POISON +50%.
- Hex Witch: ARCANE +50%, FIRE +25%, HOLY −25%, immune to curse.
- Axe Armor (iron): PHYS +30%, SHOCK −50%, immune to bleed.
- Gargoyle (stone): PHYS +25%, FIRE +50%, SHOCK −40%.
- Blood Bat: BLOOD +50%, FROST −25%.
- Gorehorn: FIRE −20%.
- Rune Colossus: ARCANE +50%, SHOCK −30%.
- Count Vlador: HOLY −50%, BLOOD +75%, FIRE +25%.
The first typed hit on each enemy pops a 'WEAK!' or 'RESIST' micro-label.
  - *Implementation:* enemyDefs.ts gets material, resist?: Partial<Record<DmgName, number>> and a statusImmune bitmask. A flat RESIST Float32Array(defs×8) is built at module load. Resist is clamped to [−1, 0.9]. On bosses, immunity downgrades to 60% resist so no build is hard-countered. In world.ts, the bony string checks (lines 483 and 559) are replaced by a MATERIAL_FX[material] lookup for particle kind and colour.
  - *Acceptance:* A Holy Cross hit deals 1.5× to a Skeleton and 1.0× to a Gorehorn (no HOLY resist). No boss has effective resist of 0.9 or more for any type. Every EnemyDef has a material. The Rune Colossus now bursts stone debris on hits instead of falling through to blood.
- **Dedicated combat & FX PRNG streams** — Separate combat randomness (crit, variance, proc chance, status rolls) from AI and spawn randomness, so a build change never alters how enemies behave. Remove Math.random from simulation modules.
  - *Implementation:* rng.ts gets STREAM.combat = 7 and STREAM.simFx = 8. World gets crng = new Rng(hashCombine(room.seed, STREAM.combat)) and fxRng, which only presentation code reads. Crit, variance, chain and status rolls move to crng. Burn particle jitter (enemyAi.ts:28) and the Math.random calls at playerCombat.ts:137/317 move to fxRng. An ESLint rule bans Math.random in src/game and src/gen.
  - *Acceptance:* With immortal enemies (hp 1e9) and knockback 0, enemy x/y after 2,400 ticks are identical whether the player has 0% or 75% crit. A run hash (FNV over enemy and projectile positions every 120 ticks) matches across two runs of the same seed and intent script. grep finds no Math.random under src/game or src/gen.
- **Effect registry replacing the closed HookKey union** — Cards, relics, items, sub-weapons and statuses register effects on typed triggers: onHit, onCrit, onKill, onHurt, onDash, onPerfectDodge, onRoomStart, onRoomClear, onTick, onStatusApplied and onReaction. A new card is then one data row plus one effect module.
  - *Implementation:* src/game/effects/index.ts holds EFFECTS: EffectDef[] (id, triggers bitmask, handlers as plain module functions). Player.recompute builds triggerLists (one Int16Array per trigger, cap 48) and effectStacks (Uint8Array(EFFECT_COUNT)) only when cards or equipment change. The 12 current hooks (fork, singularity, satellites, cross, axe, holyWater, bloodlust, burn, chain, moonDash, scythe, requiem) move into effects/*.ts. updateSubWeapons becomes a loop over onTick effects. Recursion guard: hit depth is at most 2, and DOT or NO_PROC hits never fire procs.
  - *Acceptance:* All existing sim tests pass unchanged. A stub effect receives exactly N onKill calls for N kills. A recursion test with fork, chain and burn at max stacks plus an on-crit chain card terminates without exceeding depth 2. After migration, grep finds no 'p.hooks.' outside effects/ and the renderer adapter.
- **Proc coefficients, crit-token fix & source table** — Every source carries a proc coefficient so chance-on-hit effects are fair across attack shapes: Arc Slash 1.0, Slash Wave 0.5, Homing Orb 0.8, Twin Dagger 0.45 each, Shard 0.25, Cross 0.6, Axe 1.0, Familiar 0.25, Scythe 0.5, hazards and DoT 0. Shadow Step's guaranteed crit becomes a crit token that only the player's direct attacks and class abilities can spend, never familiar ticks or Requiem.
  - *Implementation:* src/game/sources.ts holds an SRC enum and a SOURCES table {name, icon, type, procCoef, canConsumeCritToken, family}. ProjectileStore and HazardStore gain src: Uint8Array. ProjectileStore.flags widens to Uint16Array: all 6 bits are in use today, and E3 needs PF_BOUNCE and PF_CATCH. Storm Chain's chance becomes 0.2 × procCoef.
  - *Acceptance:* On a single-target dummy at 1.0 attack speed, chain procs per second for the three classes land within ±25% of each other (today Phantom procs about 3× as often as Vanguard). Test sequence Shadow Step, then a wisp tick, then a dagger: the dagger crits and the wisp does not.
- **Damage attribution & DPS ledger** — Track damage and kills per source so players can see what their build is doing, and so the game-over screen and balance tooling have real data. Burn and other DoTs finally get credit, with their damage numbers grouped into one pop every 0.5 s.
  - *Implementation:* Player gets dmgBySource: Float64Array(SRC_COUNT) and killsBySource, both persisted in PlayerSave v2 (defaults on migration). dealHit increments them. DoT numbers flush from a per-enemy accumulator every 60 ticks. UI (dependency): a bar list on the pause Character tab and the top 3 sources on the game-over screen.
  - *Acceptance:* After a full sim room, the sum of dmgBySource equals damageDealt within 0.1%. A burn-only kill increments killsBySource[BURN]. The ledger survives save and load.
- **Hit-feel layer: hit-stop, overcrit, weak-point pops** — Chunky impact feedback:
- Hit-stop of 25 ms on crits, 50 ms on elite kills, 80 ms on parries and shatters.
- Overcrit: crit chance above the 0.75 cap converts to crit damage at 1:2 and shows as a bigger number with a star.
- Skewed 'WEAK!' tags in the chunky outlined font.
  - *Implementation:* Presentation only. The sim emits fx.hitStop(ms) through FxSink (NullFx gets a stub). app.ts pauses loop accumulation by setting timeScale to 0 for that many real milliseconds, capped at 120 ms per rolling second. Fixed-step simulation and determinism are unaffected. The bitmap number pool gains a weight tier parameter. Reduced-motion mode turns hit-stop off.
  - *Acceptance:* Total hit-stop never exceeds 120 ms in any rolling 1 s window (unit test on the limiter). With reduced motion on, hit-stop is 0 ms. Overcrit test: 0.95 crit becomes 0.75 chance plus 0.40 extra crit damage.

**Depends on:** TEC: ESLint rules (no Math.random in sim, no allocation in hot files) and CI to enforce them; RENDER: type-coloured, weight-tiered bitmap damage numbers and 'WEAK!'/'RESIST' label sprites; UI: DPS ledger panel on the pause Character tab and the game-over screen; SAVE: PlayerSave/RunSave version 2 migration framework
