# Castle Atlas (v0.6) — Epoch Two: rebuilt structure, branching acts, two more biomes

[← Roadmap overview](../ROADMAP.md)


**Goal:** Ship every seed-reshaping change in one migrated release: a device-independent ruleset, the set-piece grammar and boss arenas, four-sided doors, branching acts through the Castle Atlas, a paced difficulty curve, and the Forbidden Library and Underground Reservoir biomes. Nightly bots and power budgets balance it all, and castle codes make every night shareable.

**Demo moment:** You beat the Castle Gate boss and the Castle Atlas unfolds: one portal ticks toward the Clock Tower, the other drips toward the drowned Reservoir. A friend pastes your castle code and walks the same night.

**Why now:** Bundling every reshaper (GEN-1's per-pass streams from v0.4, GEN-3 with BIO-4's prefabs, GEN-8's doors, BIO-2's acts, BAL-4's path guarantees) into one epoch means seed history and dailies break once, not seven times. BIO-2 only matters with two biomes per tier, so BIO-5 (absorbing ENM-8's rosters) ships with it. GEN-2's quality gates validate the new archetypes. TEC-7 (absorbing BAL-2) and BAL-3 must run before the big monster and card waves so new content is priced when it comes in. SOC-2 owns the code format that BIO-2's route codes reuse.

**Estimate:** ~14 weeks

**Exit criteria:**

- [ ] Epoch Two is the default, with one generation-epoch bump. Seed labels and castle codes carry the epoch and build fingerprint, the epoch-1 generator lazy-loads for one release, and old seed-history entries still replay.
- [ ] Streams for secrets, boss pools, captives and lore are reserved in the registry, so post-launch content never forces a third epoch. Seeded boss-pool draws ship with single-entry pools.
- [ ] A nightly 200k-seed fuzz run shows zero connectivity, safe-route or spawn-fairness failures, and p99 room generation is ≤ 8 ms on the reference phone.
- [ ] A Full Moon run takes four acts (Gate → {Catacombs | Clock Tower} → {Library | Reservoir} → Throne) in ~30–35 minutes. The Castle Atlas pause tab works, and castle codes share as a link, share sheet or QR. Only four-sided doors ship from GEN-8; daises, chasms and Long March halls are not in scope.
- [ ] Kiter, Brawler and Greedy bots play 1,000 seeded runs nightly across 3 classes. Boss share of floor enemy HP is ≤ 40%, level-ups per floor sit within band, and CI rejects any card, item or enemy without a budget value.
- [ ] The same daily seed produces identical tapes and hashes on desktop Chromium, iOS Safari and Android Chrome.

#### GAP-2 · GOV-2 Epoch Two: one coordinated seed rebaseline and a device-independent ruleset

*Gaps found in review · effort L · impact 5/5*

Ship every seed-reshaping change in a single migrated release, under one rule: the sim never reads the device. Enemy caps, view rects and assist constants move out of the quality presets into a fixed ruleset. Weak devices get presentation-only LOD. Transcendental math goes through a deterministic dmath module. Seeds, dailies and labels carry an epoch, and the epoch-1 generator stays on a lazy-loaded shelf for one release. **Player value:** A seed shared from a phone plays identically on a 4K desktop. Dailies and leaderboards are fair across devices. Existing seeds and runs in progress survive the big content update, instead of being invalidated five separate times.

- **Ruleset replaces device caps** — RULESET.standard fixes simEnemyCap at 160 alive enemies. Surplus enemies queue and arrive as extra trickle waves when the alive count drops, instead of folding into hpMul. The ruleset also defines a fixed logical view rect (CONFIG.viewTilesW×H) for any sim rule that depends on 'on screen', and device-independent aim-assist constants. World takes a ruleset, and applyPreset no longer touches the sim. Locking this in now is cheap: today's room budgets (about 14 × 2.2 × 1.25 ≈ 38 cost units) rarely reach even the Low cap of 80.
  - *Implementation:* New src/game/ruleset.ts. world.ts takes a ruleset instead of enemyCap and owns the overflow wave logic; spawner.ts queues overflow instead of folding it. app.ts:165/353/365 stop passing CONFIG.enemyCap. CONFIG.enemyCap is renamed renderActorBudget and moves into PRESETS.
  - *Acceptance:* A parameterised vitest runs the same seed and intent tape under the low, medium, high and ultra presets and the touch, KBM and gamepad methods; all give identical state hashes after 10,000 ticks. A grep test finds no CONFIG.enemyCap or preset reads under src/game or src/gen. A thermal step-down mid-room leaves the state hash unchanged.
- **Presentation-only LOD for weak devices** — Weak devices stay smooth without the sim changing. The renderer culls outside the camera and uses crowd tiers: beyond N on-screen actors, distant ones drop their flash overlay, shadow and secondary parts. Damage numbers coalesce per target, and particle budgets scale with the preset.
  - *Implementation:* renderer.ts actor loop picks a lodTier from PresetSpec.actorLod. shapes.ts adds a lite context per rig (body without detail strokes, built once). particles.ts applies the budgets. fxSink.ts coalesces damage per enemy every 6 ticks.
  - *Acceptance:* In a Playwright perf scenario under 4× Chromium CPU throttling at 412×915 and DPR 2.6, a scripted 160-enemy room on the Low preset averages at least 55 FPS. The sim state hash is identical with LOD on and off.
- **dmath: engine-independent transcendental math** — Sim and generation code take sin, cos, atan2, hypot, exp and pow from a deterministic module built only from IEEE-exact operations (+, −, ×, ÷ and sqrt). Its tables are generated at boot from fixed-coefficient polynomials, never from Math.sin, so the tables themselves come out identical on every engine.
  - *Implementation:* src/core/dmath.ts: a 4096-entry sin table built from a minimax polynomial with linear interpolation, a polynomial atan2, and hypot via sqrt. A codemod converts the trig calls in src/game (about 69 lines) and poisson.ts:57-58. ESLint no-restricted-properties bans Math.sin/cos/atan2/hypot/exp/pow/log in src/game and src/gen.
  - *Acceptance:* A Playwright job hashes 10^6 dmath evaluations in Chromium, Firefox, WebKit and Node, and all four hashes match. Maximum absolute error is 2e-6 or less. The heavy tick regresses by no more than 10%. The lint rule blocks a new Math.sin in src/game.
- **Epoch gate and golden seed corpus** — Make seed-reshaping changes visible and deliberate. A corpus of 200 seeds × 3 floors × 2 attempts hashes the graph, tiles, spawn plans, loot, and shop and event rolls. Any hash change fails CI unless the same PR bumps EPOCH. The Epoch 2 release train bundles every queued reshaper: the registry swaps for the ad-hoc salts, GEN-1 per-pass streams, the GEN-3 pickEvent reorder, dmath in Poisson, BIO-2 acts, BAL-4 path guarantees, ENM-7 boss pools, GEN-6 and GEN-8 graph changes, and the ruleset from the first feature.
  - *Implementation:* src/core/epoch.ts holds the EPOCH const. tests/golden/seeds.e1.json and seeds.e2.json hold the corpora, checked by tests/epoch.test.ts. This epic owns an epoch-2 integration branch, and reshaping PRs merge dark behind REL-1 flags.
  - *Acceptance:* Changing the Poisson radius without bumping EPOCH turns CI red. The epoch-2 corpus is committed next to the epoch-1 corpus. Every reshaping epic's acceptance criteria point at the epoch-2 corpus instead of a version tag of its own.
- **Epoch-aware seeds, labels and daily history** — Seed labels become `2·ABC1234-0`, and legacy labels without an epoch parse as epoch 1. Daily keys include the epoch (`daily:e2:2026-10-14`), SeedRecord stores it, and the history and daily screens mark epoch-1 entries as 'Old Castle'.
  - *Implementation:* core/rng.ts seedToString/seedFromString handle {epoch, seed, attempt}. run.ts seedLabel includes the epoch. meta.ts SeedRecord gains an epoch field, migrated to default 1. The daily derivation at app.ts:278 adds the epoch, and ui/screens.ts shows a badge in the history list.
  - *Acceptance:* Labels round-trip for both epochs, and 'ABC1234-0' resolves to epoch 1. The same date produces different daily seeds in epoch 1 and epoch 2. A migrated MetaSave fixture keeps all 20 history entries, each tagged epoch 1.
- **Legacy generator shelf and stale-run handling** — The epoch-1 graph, room, spawner, Poisson and loot code is frozen into a lazy-loaded chunk for one release, so mid-run saves and epoch-1 seeds can still be finished. Once the shelf is removed, an epoch-1 save offers 'Open in archived castle' (the REL-1 archive URL) or 'Abandon and keep souls as if fled'.
  - *Implementation:* src/gen/legacy/e1/* is a frozen copy, never edited and eslint-ignored. run.ts picks a generator by header.epoch via dynamic import(). app.ts continueRun shows the stale-run dialog. Vite manualChunks keeps the legacy code out of the main chunk.
  - *Acceptance:* An epoch-1 RunSave fixture regenerates room tiles byte-identical to the committed fixtures. A bundle test confirms the legacy chunk is 15 KB gzipped or less and absent from the main chunk. With the shelf flag off, an epoch-1 save shows the stale-run dialog.
- **Cross-device determinism CI** — A nightly and pre-release job replays scripted runs (3 per class, all 3 floors, from TEC-7 bots or recorded tapes) in Node, Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, under every preset and input method, and compares per-room state hashes.
  - *Implementation:* e2e/determinism.spec.ts (Playwright) loads ?replay=<tape> with rendering disabled. Tapes live in tests/fixtures/tapes/*.bin. The hash is FNV over the SoA stores, the player and the RNG states, taken at each room exit.
  - *Acceptance:* Every engine and preset produces the same list of hashes. The job finishes in under 8 minutes. A canary test confirms that a deliberately injected Math.random in src/game gets caught.

**Depends on:** GOV-1 Keystone Contracts (stream registry, RunHeader); REL-1 Release Train (flags to merge reshapers dark, archived builds); TEC-1 Night Watch CI; TEC-3 Chronicle of Blood (state hash, tapes); GEN-1, GEN-3, GEN-6, GEN-8, BIO-2, BAL-4, ENM-7 (reshapers bundled into the epoch)
**Risks:** Holding reshaping PRs for the epoch train delays visible content. Mitigate by merging them dark behind flags and timing the train with PRD-1's slice.; Capping at 160 with a queue changes the desktop Ultra feel compared with the theoretical 400 cap. BAL-4 must re-tune its pacing bands.; dmath can shift projectile arcs slightly and needs a feel pass.; Playwright's WebKit on Linux is not iOS Safari's JIT tier, so real-device spot checks stay in TEC-8's lab.
**Constraint check:** This epic exists to deliver the determinism constraint across devices. The sim budget holds: a 160 cap is close to the measured 150-enemy tick of about 0.3 ms in Node. dmath tables are generated from code at boot, using about 32 KB of memory and adding no data to the bundle. The legacy shelf is a lazy chunk, so the main bundle doesn't grow. LOD is render-only, so all four presets and every platform share one sim.

#### GEN-2 · Castle Surveyor: generation quality metrics, fuzzing and fairness guarantees

*Procedural generation technology · effort M · impact 5/5*

Turn 'it's connected' into measurable standards. Every room gets metrics, quality gates with deterministic rerolls, spawn rules based on path distance and line of sight, safety checks on wave spawn points, and a fuzz harness that runs 200,000 seeds every night. **Player value:** No more archer sniping you from across the room the instant you enter, and no bat flock spawning inside your dash. Room pacing is consistent, so every hall feels designed rather than diced.

- **Room metrics module** — Add measureRoom(room) to produce a RoomMetrics record covering playability and pacing.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/gen/metrics.ts. Metrics: floorRatio; distance-transform clearance histogram and largest open square; corridorShare (walkable tiles with clearance 0 or 1 and exactly 2 opposite neighbours); deadEnds; spawn-to-exit BFS lengths and tortuosity (BFS distance over Manhattan distance); loops (cyclomatic number of the 2x2-coarsened walkable graph); chokepoints (Tarjan articulation points on the coarse graph); coverDensity (LOW tiles and pillars per 100 floor tiles); decorDensity; landmarkCount. All of it uses GenScratch buffers.
  - *Acceptance:* Fixture tests: a 10x10 open box gives floorRatio 1, 0 dead ends and 0 loops. A ring corridor gives exactly 1 loop. A known L-corridor gives the expected tortuosity. A full measurement of a 60x40 room takes at most 0.5 ms in Node.
- **Quality gates with deterministic rerolls** — Reject rooms outside per-kind bands and regenerate them from a derived seed, so the result is still reproducible.
  - *Implementation:* Add CONFIG.genQuality. combat: floorRatio [0.36, 0.58], exitPath [40, 100], largestOpenSquare ≥ 7, deadEnds ≤ 3, loops ≥ 1 when w ≥ 48, cover [1, 5] per 100 floor tiles. elite: largestOpenSquare ≥ 8. Small rooms: floorRatio [0.4, 0.7]. The pipeline retries with hashCombine(roomSeed, REROLL, k) for k ≤ 4, then falls back to the 'Great Hall' archetype (Epic 3), which is known to pass. The reroll index goes into the room's debug info.
  - *Acceptance:* Across 20,000 rooms, 100% pass the gates. Reroll rate is under 8% and fallback rate under 0.1%. The combat spawn-to-exit spread narrows from 46–119 to 40–100 tiles.
- **Spawn fairness v2 (path distance and line of sight)** — Enforce distance by walking path, not straight line. Give ranged enemies no line of sight to the arrival pad, and make sure every enemy needs some time to reach the player.
  - *Implementation:* In spawner.ts compute a BFS distance field from spawn once (scratch). Rules: melee at least 8 path tiles; Blood Bat (165 px/s, about 5.2 tiles/s) at least 10; elites at least 12; Bone Archer, Hex Witch and Gargoyle get no LOS (using the SIGHT table) to the 3x3 spawn pad within 12 tiles; every wave-1 entry has arrival time pathTiles*32/speed ≥ 1.8 s (entry invulnerability is 1 s); the spawn pad is flagged MF_NOSPAWN. The Poisson accept callback uses these checks, and rejected entries fall back to the farthest legal sockets.
  - *Acceptance:* A 10,000-room combat/elite fuzz run shows 0 violations. Today the minimum path is 6 tiles, 9.8% of rooms have an enemy within 8 tiles, and 4.3% have a ranged enemy with LOS. A dedicated test asserts the minimum wave-1 BFS distance is at least 8.
- **Runtime safety for wave spawn points** — Later waves never materialise on top of the player or inside an active hazard.
  - *Implementation:* planSpawns emits 3 candidate points per entry (a primary plus 2 alternates, all precomputed and stored in a Float32Array). World.startWave picks the first candidate at least 5 tiles from the player's current position and outside live TELEGRAPH/WELL hazards. If none qualifies, it delays that telegraph by 0.3 s and retries. No allocation, and no wall-clock input.
  - *Acceptance:* A sim test parks the player on every wave-2 primary point: no enemy appears within 5 tiles. Two runs with identical intents produce identical enemy positions.
- **Floor pacing validator** — Graph-level fairness: every route gets breathing room, no route is a meat-grinder, and elites are actually reachable.
  - *Implementation:* In src/gen/graph.ts enumerate all start-to-boss paths (at most 3^5 = 243). Rules: each path has at least one rest, shop or event before the pre-boss column; at most 3 consecutive combat nodes on any path; floors 1–2 have at least one elite on at least one path; puzzle and challenge caps from Epic 7. Repair is deterministic (retype the lowest-id offender from the graph RNG).
  - *Acceptance:* 10,000 graphs satisfy all old and new invariants. Node-type shares stay within ±2 points of the CONFIG.nodeWeights targets.
- **Fuzz harness, invariant registry and shrinker** — One registry of invariants, run quickly in CI and at scale overnight, with a reproducible report when one fails.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/gen/invariants.ts covering connectivity (including one-way edges from Epic 8), props on legal tiles, door pads clear, traps off the safe route, secrets reachable only through BREAKABLE tiles, keys before vaults, puzzles solvable, spawn fairness and gen time p99. tests/fuzz.gen.test.ts runs 2,000 seeds in under 20 s. scripts/fuzz.ts (vite-node, `npm run fuzz -- --seeds 200000`) prints histograms and the worst seeds. On failure it prints {runSeed, floor, attempt, nodeId, GEN_VERSION} and an ASCII dump of the room.
  - *Acceptance:* With a test-only flag that skips validateConnectivity or the trap safe-route pass, the harness flags the bug within 500 seeds. The nightly run of 200,000 seeds finishes in under 10 minutes on CI.
- **Developer generation atlas and in-game overlay** — A visual inspector so designers can see spawn heatmaps, LOS cones, trap coverage and safe routes.
  - *Implementation:* scripts/genAtlas.ts writes a self-contained HTML file with an inline SVG grid of 64 rooms plus metric overlays. `?debug=gen&seed=...` draws the same layers in-game with Pixi Graphics behind import.meta.env.DEV, so it is tree-shaken from production.
  - *Acceptance:* Production bundle size changes by at most 1 KB. The atlas for 64 rooms builds in under 10 s. The overlay shows BFS distance and LOS for the current room.

**Depends on:** TEC: CI pipeline with a nightly fuzz job; AI: enemy speed and LOS constants stay data-driven in enemyDefs.ts; REN: debug overlay layer
**Risks:** Over-tight gates raise reroll rates and generation time. Watch p99 time alongside pass rates.; Choosing alternate wave points at runtime couples spawning to player position. It is deterministic given the same inputs, but future replay validation must record inputs.; Fuzz runtime can grow as content grows. Keep the quick CI tier capped at 20 s.
**Constraint check:** Code-only. Metrics and solvers run at generation time or in tests, never in the 120 Hz tick. Runtime wave-point selection reads preallocated Float32Arrays. All dev tools are behind DEV flags or live in scripts/, so the shipped bundle is unaffected. Deterministic through derived reroll seeds.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (keep): Cheap and high leverage. Absorb BAL-1's safe wave spawns. Picking wave points at runtime depends on player position, which is replay-safe only while positions come purely from recorded intents.
- *player* (keep): Cheap insurance that every generated room is fair; invisible but prevents bad runs. Share spawn-fairness rules with BAL-1 so there is one owner of 'no cheap spawns'.
- *completeness* (keep, effort L): High value. Fuzzer, shrinker, metrics, atlas and overlay add up to L. Share infrastructure with TEC-7, and budget CI minutes for 200k nightly seeds.

#### GEN-3 · Gothic Blueprints: a set-piece grammar and room archetypes

*Procedural generation technology · effort L · impact 5/5*

A code-defined ASCII prefab grammar (strings in TypeScript, not asset files) compiled into stampable set-pieces, plus archetype recipes such as Great Hall, Crypt Warren, Library Stacks, Chapel Nave, Moonlit Ballroom, Armory Gallery and Collapsed Bridge. Rooms become recognisable castle places with landmarks and names, and each boss gets an arena built for its moves. **Player value:** Rooms become memorable places like 'the Weeping Nave of Saint Ambrose', with pews to hide behind, a pipe-organ loft or a bell to ring. The Count's throne room finally looks like a throne room, and Gorehorn smashes pillars in his own larder.

- **ASCII set-piece DSL and compiler** — Set-pieces are written as glyph rows in TypeScript and compiled at module load into typed arrays, with variant groups, optional cells and mirror/rotation transforms.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/gen/setpieces/dsl.ts with sp({id, rows, tags, floors, kinds, weight, transforms}). Glyphs: # wall, . floor, , carpet, o pillar, = low wall (pew/shelf/balustrade), _ pit, + bridge, x breakable, ^ spike plate, | pendulum anchor, * chandelier anchor, i candle, u urn, b barrel, c coffin, t torch slot, w window slot, E/L/R/A enemy sockets (any / elite / ranged perch / armor alcove), k key pedestal, V vault gate, $ treasure, ! landmark anchor, D door socket, 1–9 variant groups (one chosen per group), ? 50% optional. Output is CompiledSetPiece {w, h, tiles, meta, props, sockets} per allowed transform (mirrorX, mirrorY, rot180; rot90 only for pieces tagged ROTATABLE, with door sockets remapped). Content lives in src/gen/setpieces/{gate,catacombs,throne,boss,common}.ts.
  - *Acceptance:* The compile test checks every piece: rows are rectangular; an unknown glyph errors with piece id, row and column; sockets are connected in every variant and transform. At least 40 pieces are registered (12 landmark, 16 filler, 3 boss arena, 5 small-room, 4 vault/secret). The compiled tables add at most 12 KB gzipped.
- **Stamp and stitch with protected prefabs** — Place set-pieces into BSP leaves and connect them only through their sockets. Corridors and the connectivity repair pass can never cut through a prefab.
  - *Implementation:* A new 'setpiece' pass picks leaves with w ≥ piece.w+2 and h ≥ piece.h+2, stamps tiles, meta (MF_SETPIECE) and props, and registers socket anchors. connectSubtrees() uses the sockets as anchors. astar() takes a blocked mask: MF_SETPIECE costs Infinity except at sockets. validateConnectivity may only carve outside MF_SETPIECE. If stitching fails, the pass un-stamps and carves a plain rectangle instead (deterministic).
  - *Acceptance:* Over 5,000 rooms, a per-piece checksum of stamped tiles equals the compiled tiles (0 cuts). The connectivity invariant holds. At least 95% of combat rooms contain a set-piece.
- **Combat room archetypes** — Seven archetype recipes give each room a shape identity, weighted by floor.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/gen/archetypes.ts with entries {id, kinds, floors, weight, params:{bspMinLeaf, cavernFraction, corridorWidth, requiredPieces, decorRuleSet, zoneTheme}}. The seven: Great Hall (a 40x9 carpeted central nave with a pillar every 4 tiles and BSP side chapels); Crypt Warren (cavernFraction 0.6, coffin niches, 2-wide tunnels); Library Stacks (2x8 TILE_LOW shelf rows with 2-wide gaps every 5 tiles, so archers shoot over shelves); Chapel Nave (mirrorX symmetry, pews, altar landmark, rose window); Moonlit Ballroom (30x20 open marble floor with 3 chandelier anchors); Armory Gallery (alcoves of dormant Axe Armor statues that wake in wave 2); Collapsed Bridge (a chasm band crossed by 2 bridges). Castle Gate weights: Great Hall 30, Library 20, Chapel 20, Armory 15, Courtyard Ruins 15.
  - *Acceptance:* Archetype distribution over 10,000 rooms is within ±15% of the weights. Each archetype passes the Epic 2 quality gates on the first try at least 92% of the time.
- **Bespoke boss arenas** — Each boss gets an arena that feeds its pattern set instead of the shared octagon.
  - *Implementation:* In src/gen/setpieces/boss.ts, BOSS_ARENA[floor] replaces the hard-coded boss branch in generateRoom(). Gorehorn's Larder: 46x34 with 2 breakable meat-hook pillars (TILE_BREAKABLE; one charge hit turns them to rubble, so cover shrinks as the fight goes on) and a cauldron landmark whose rim sockets spawn his summons. Rune Foundry for the Rune Colossus: a circular rune floor drawn as a zone glyph, 4 rune pylons as LOW cover, and spike sockets aligned to the rune lines. Blood-Moon Throne for Count Vlador: mirrored, with an MF_RAISED throne dais to the east, a rose window, 6 candelabras for an AI 'snuff the lights' phase, 4 bat-roost sockets and 8 teleport sockets, each at least 6 tiles from walls. Sockets are exposed in RoomMap.sockets.
  - *Acceptance:* Each boss room uses its own arena 100% of the time. Teleport sockets are reachable and at least 6 tiles from the spawn pad. The existing 'bosses run all their patterns' sim test passes.
- **Bespoke small rooms** — Start, shop, rest and event rooms become authored-feeling spaces, and each mystery event gets matching architecture.
  - *Implementation:* Start rooms per floor: Drawbridge Gate, Ossuary Stair and Throne Antechamber. Shop: Merchant's Lantern Nook (merchant socket, crates, rug). Rest: Sanctuary Chapel (campfire and anvil sockets 5 tiles apart, kneeling statues, stained glass). pickEvent() in economy.ts runs before layout so the event picks its room: blood_altar → Blood Altar Apse, fortune_skull → Velvet Parlour, cursed_armory → Forgotten Armory, wandering_ghost → Misty Portrait Gallery, holy_fountain → Moonlit Fountain Court. World.loadRoom places interactables at sockets instead of at feature ± 2.5 tiles.
  - *Acceptance:* Event id to room piece mapping is 100%. Interactable sockets are FLOOR with clearance 1. Small rooms no longer share layouts across kinds.
- **Landmarks and room names** — Every combat or elite room gets one landmark of at least 8x6 and a generated gothic name, with no repeats within a floor.
  - *Implementation:* At Run.enterFloor, passRng(fSeed, LANDMARK) pre-assigns landmarks to combat and elite nodes from the floor pool without replacement, so the result doesn't depend on visit order. Pool: Blood Fountain, Great Bell, Pipe Organ Loft, Gargoyle Perch, Hanging Cage Row, Hunter's Statue, Crimson Tapestry Wall, Bone Throne. The name generator follows 'The {adj} {archetype} of {saint/noun}' (for example 'The Weeping Nave of Saint Ambrose') and feeds the HUD room label and map tooltip (UI).

- **Encounter sockets and a geometry-aware spawner** — Enemies spawn where their kit makes sense: archers on perches, Axe Armors with charge lanes, bats near windows, Ghouls bursting from coffins.
  - *Implementation:* spawner.ts scores candidate tiles per enemy AI type using sockets and meta. Ranged and caster enemies prefer R perches or tiles behind TILE_LOW. Chargers prefer tiles with a straight clear lane of at least 10 tiles (per-axis run lengths precomputed in scratch). Flutter enemies prefer roost sockets or tiles under windows. Ghouls take coffin props. Poisson sampling fills the remaining entries. Everything stays on STREAM.enemies.
  - *Acceptance:* When perches exist, at least 70% of Bone Archers spawn on or behind cover. At least 90% of Axe Armors have a 10-tile lane. Epic 2 fairness invariants still show 0 violations.

**Depends on:** Epic 1 (pipeline, meta layer, props, setTile); Epic 2 (quality gates validate archetypes); REN: zone-themed floor patterns (marble, planks, crypt slabs) and rune-circle glyph; ART: pews, shelves, cauldron, organ, bell, throne and statues as shared GraphicsContexts; AI: bosses consume arena sockets (teleport points, candelabra snuff phase, pillar smashing); UI: room name label and map tooltip
**Risks:** Prefab-heavy rooms can feel repetitive. Keep at least 16 filler pieces per floor, plus variant groups and transforms (about 4x apparent variety).; Protected prefabs make A* stitching fail more often in small leaves. The plain-rectangle fallback and the reroll budget cover this, but watch reroll rate.; Moving pickEvent before layout changes event-room seeds. Bundle this with the GEN_VERSION 2 rebaseline.
**Constraint check:** Set-pieces are TypeScript string literals compiled to Uint8Arrays at load, so there are no asset files. About 40 pieces at roughly 150 bytes each stays under 12 KB gzipped. Stamping is O(piece area) at generation time only. Determinism comes from per-pass streams. Visuals reuse shared GraphicsContexts per prop kind. Nothing is input-specific.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (keep): Absorbs BIO-4. Moving pickEvent before layout reshapes event rooms, so ride the GEN_VERSION bump. Protected prefabs need GEN-2's reroll budget, measured at p99 on phones.
- *player* (keep): Named rooms and landmarks make the castle feel authored. Owns all prefabs (absorbs BIO-4) and doors-on-any-side. Bundle its seed change into the single GEN_VERSION bump.

#### BIO-4 · 4. Set-piece rooms and prefab stamps

*Biomes, run structure & world · effort L · impact 4/5*

Mix procedural generation with hand-authored rooms. A small ASCII prefab format, written as strings in TypeScript, stamps signature chambers (the Grand Staircase, the Clock Face Chamber, the Library Rotunda, the Flooded Cistern) into BSP layouts. #### GEN-8 · Grand Halls: four-sided doors, multi-screen rooms and depth illusions

*Procedural generation technology · effort XL · impact 4/5*

Break the rule that you always enter from the west and exit east. Doors go on any side, Long March halls advance section by section behind portcullises, raised throne daises and balconies fake height, and chasms show the floor below in parallax. All of it stays a flat tile grid underneath. **Player value:** The castle feels huge and vertical: a five-screen marching corridor, archers firing from balustraded balconies, a bridge over a moonlit abyss, and Shockwave-dashing a Skeleton off a ledge.

- **Four-sided door topology and trigger rectangles** — Exits go on north, south or east edges depending on graph geometry. Exits are triggered by per-door rectangles instead of an east-edge x test.
  - *Implementation:* DoorInfo.side becomes 'N' | 'S' | 'E' | 'W'. The side comes from the row-fraction difference between nodes: a delta of 0.2 or more gives S, −0.2 or less gives N, anything else gives E. Several doors per side are spaced at least 6 tiles apart. World.checkExit tests world-space trigger rectangles. RoomView badges and door art rotate (ART). The fade transition slides in the door's direction (REN).
  - *Acceptance:* Across 10,000 rooms, every door is on its computed side, reachable, has a clear 3x3 pad, and exits are at least 4 tiles apart. A sim test walking through a north door emits an exit event with the right target.
- **Late-bound entrance pass** — The layout stays independent of how the player arrived. Only the entrance door, spawn pad and wave placement depend on the edge walked.
  - *Implementation:* generateRoom(seed, kind, exits, floor, entrySide) runs the layout passes without entrySide, then an 'entrance' pass with its own stream places the sealed door on the side opposite the traversed exit, picks the spawn pad near it and runs the fairness checks. planSpawns hashes entrySide into its seed. RunSave already stores visited, so the path is reproducible.
  - *Acceptance:* The tile hash before the entrance pass is identical for every entrySide. Spawn fairness invariants hold for all 4 sides over 10,000 rooms.
- **Long March multi-section halls** — A Castlevania-style corridor about 5 screens wide, split into sections by portcullises that rise as each section is cleared.
  - *Implementation:* A 'longMarch' archetype of 120x28 tiles, at most 1 per floor, weight 8 on floors 1–2. TILE_GATE rows split it into 3–4 sections of about 30 tiles. planSpawns returns sections: SpawnPlan[], and World tracks sectionIdx. Clearing a section calls setTile on the gate rows (FLOOR) with a rising-portcullis effect. The enemy cap applies per section. The camera looks ahead in the travel direction (REN).
  - *Acceptance:* Sections clear in order. No enemy from section k spawns before gate k−1 opens. The perf test on a 120x28 room stays under 0.4 ms per tick with 140 enemies. Flow-field BFS over 3,360 tiles stays at or under 0.05 ms.
- **Raised daises, balconies and stairs** — Fake height with MF_RAISED zones ringed by balustrades (TILE_LOW), reachable only by stairs. Archers on balconies shoot over the rails.
  - *Implementation:* In set-pieces and archetypes, '/' marks stair tiles and MF_RAISED marks raised zones. RoomView draws raised floors with a +16 px offset, a two-tile-tall south face and cast shadows (REN). The balustrade is TILE_LOW: WALK = 0, SHOT and SIGHT = 1, so projectiles cross it but walkers must use the stairs. The spawner prefers R perches on balconies. Metrics count the balcony's cover.
  - *Acceptance:* A projectile crosses a balustrade and a Skeleton cannot (sim test). Every raised zone has at least 1 stair connected to the main region. Archers spawn on balconies at least 60% of the time when one exists.
- **One-way ledge drops with directed connectivity** — Ledges you can drop off but can't climb back up, validated so they can never softlock the player.
  - *Implementation:* TILE_DROP_S is walkable only with southward motion: World.slide passes the movement sign, and Y movement into DROP is allowed only when dy > 0. Reverse-BFS flow for enemies allows c→n only if n→c is legal. The validate pass checks directed reachability: from every walkable tile, every exit and the boss must be reachable. Repair carves stairs.
  - *Acceptance:* Over 10,000 rooms with ledges, every walkable tile can reach every exit (no softlocks). Enemies on low ground never path up a ledge (sim test).
- **Chasms, bridges and a parallax underfloor** — Pits split rooms and are crossed on bridges. Knockback can throw enemies in. The abyss shows the next floor scrolling below.
  - *Implementation:* TILE_PIT: WALK = 0, FLY, SHOT and SIGHT = 1. World.slide gets a forced flag for knockback and dash, so a forced move can end over a pit and the entity falls. Non-elite walkers die (shrink-and-scream effect). Elites lose 35% max HP and respawn on the nearest safe tile. The player loses 12% max HP and respawns at the last safe tile with 1 s of invulnerability. Shockwave Dash's 'Pit Kick' becomes a synergy. REN masks pit regions over a parallax layer at 0.55× camera speed showing the floor below: Catacomb bones under Castle Gate, a blood-moon sky under the Throne bridges.
  - *Acceptance:* No spawn, loot, prop or door is placed on a pit. The last-safe-tile unit test passes. A forced knockback into a pit kills a Skeleton (sim). The flow field never routes walkers into pits.
- **Large-room budgets and camera hints** — Support rooms up to 128x64 with chunk culling, scaled minimaps and set-piece camera zones, keeping the mobile budget.
  - *Implementation:* Use the Epic 1 chunked RoomView with view-rectangle culling. Scale the minimap by room aspect. Set-pieces may declare camera zones (for example, zoom out 1.15× in the Ballroom, lock vertical scroll in the Long March) that REN's camera reads. Size the flow-field and spatial-hash buffers from a 128x64 maximum at World construction, so nothing reallocates per room.
  - *Acceptance:* A 128x64 room on the mobile profile holds 60 FPS in the headless Pixel 7 emulation with at most +3 draw calls compared with 60x40. No typed-array reallocation happens on room load after the first.

**Depends on:** Epic 1 (capability tables, setTile, chunked RoomView); Epic 2 (directed-connectivity invariant, fairness for every entry side); Epic 3 (archetypes and camera-zone set-pieces); REN: rotated doors, raised-floor rendering, pit parallax masks, camera zones and look-ahead; ART: portcullis, balustrade, stairs, bridge planks; AI: forced knockback into pits, balcony archer behaviour, reverse-BFS flow with one-way edges; UI: SVG map placing doors by side
**Risks:** One-way drops plus axis-separated sliding can produce edge-case snagging at corners. Needs exhaustive slide tests.; Larger rooms raise the GPU fill cost of floor contexts at 4K. Chunk culling is mandatory.; Entry-dependent spawn pads complicate golden tests. Add entrySide to the golden tuple.
**Constraint check:** Height is purely an illusion on the 2D grid: draw offsets and LOW/DROP tiles, with no 3D. The parallax underfloor is vector silhouettes in a masked container. The sim stays SoA and allocation-free, with buffers sized once for a 128x64 maximum. Determinism holds because entry side and sections come from saved path data and pass streams. **Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (revise): Ship four-sided doors first (GEN-3 and GEN-6 need them); defer daises, one-way ledges and chasm parallax. Directed connectivity breaks the symmetric BFS flow field and slide-collision assumptions.

#### BAL-4 · The Night Clock: pacing and difficulty curve

*Game design, balance & difficulty · effort L · impact 5/5*

Shape each floor as a rising night: rooms ramp up, waves leave room to breathe, bosses become a climax instead of 70-80% of the floor's enemy HP, and the XP curve hands out enough level-ups to build something. **Player value:** 25-32 minute runs with steady build growth (7-8 level-up picks instead of about 4), no dead minutes hunting a bat stuck behind a wall, and boss fights that escalate in clear phases.

- **Per-floor difficulty table** — Replace floorDifficulty, floorHpMul (1+0.55f) and floorDmgMul (1+0.32f) with one table per floor: budget, HP, damage, elite chance, waves, projectile speed, fire rate, XP and gem multipliers. It supports any number of floors, so content can add floors. Within a floor, budget ramps by column: x(0.8 + 0.4 x col/(cols-1)). Starting values for floors 1-3: budget 14/21/29, HP x1/1.5/2.0, damage x1/1.25/1.55.
  - *Implementation:* config.ts: CONFIG.curve: FloorCurve[]. spawner.ts takes node.col and the column count from Run. world.spawnEnemy reads hpMul and dmgMul from the curve. enemyDefs.ts floorHpMul and floorDmgMul become thin wrappers.
  - *Acceptance:* Spawner unit test: per-column budgets increase monotonically. Adding a floor is a data-only change. Room tile fingerprints in gen.test are unchanged.
- **Pacing targets and boss share** — Targets for the Average bot on Hunter: combat room 35-60 s, elite 50-80 s, floor 6-8 min, run 25-32 min. A boss should be at most 50% of its floor's total enemy HP (today 80% / 75% / 69%). Floors 2 and up get 6-7 columns, so a path averages at least 3 battles (today about 2.5).
  - *Implementation:* graph.ts reads per-floor column ranges from the curve. New tests/pacing.test.ts samples the generator without running the sim (planSpawns HP sums along random paths). Epic 2 supplies timings.
  - *Acceptance:* The sampling test shows boss share of at most 50% on every floor. Bot median times fall inside the bands.
- **XP curve retune** — Target 7-8 level-ups before the final boss in a 3-floor run, plus 2 per added floor. Starting values: xpBase 100 to 75, xpExp 1.45 to 1.35, kill-XP floor bonus 0.25 to 0.35. The Wandering Ghost event gives 60% of xpToNext instead of a flat 160. The final boss's XP converts to souls (1 soul per 20 XP) instead of being wasted.
  - *Implementation:* config.ts xp fields; world.killEnemy XP formula; economy.ts event effect; meta.ts soul conversion.
  - *Acceptance:* A generator-sampling test puts the mean level after each floor within +/-0.5 of [4, 6.5, 8]. Bot-measured levels are within +/-0.75.
- **Anti-stall 'Hunting Horn'** — Blood Bats and flying ranged enemies use the flow field when they lack line of sight, instead of pinning against walls. After 15 s with at most 2 enemies left, the survivors are 'Marked': a lantern icon and HUD arrow appear, they move 40% faster, and they stop keeping their kiting distance. After 30 s they dissolve and re-emerge through a spawn telegraph within 6 tiles of the player.

#### BAL-2 · Bloodhound: headless balance bots, telemetry and dashboard

*Game design, balance & difficulty · effort L · impact 5/5*

A set of headless bot players that run thousands of seeded games overnight, so config.ts is tuned against data instead of guesses. **Player value:** Indirect but foundational. Class win rates, boss fights and the economy get tuned to targets, and balance regressions are caught before players notice.

- **Extract the run controller from app.ts** — Move run orchestration (level-up offers, chests, shrines, shop, forge, campfire, events, item swaps, floor transitions, end of run) out of the DOM-bound app.ts into a headless controller. It raises typed decisions that either the UI or a bot answers.
  - *Implementation:* New src/game/runController.ts takes over openLevelUp, cardChoice, onInteract, offerItem, applyEvent, nextFloor, endRun and shopCache from app.ts. It emits Decision requests ({kind:'card', offers} | {kind:'item', item, current} | {kind:'shop', stock} | {kind:'event', ev} | {kind:'stats', points} | {kind:'path', next}) to a DecisionPolicy interface. app.ts becomes a UiPolicy built on screens.ts.
  - *Acceptance:* All existing tests pass. A full 3-floor run completes in Node with no DOM. A recorded play session gives the same end state through the controller as through the current app.ts flow.
- **Bot pilots with skill levels** — Bots that dodge projectiles, kite at their class's preferred range, loot, and walk to exits, in three skill levels. Novice: 0.25 s reaction, 30% perception noise. Average: 0.15 s, 15% noise. Expert: 0.06 s, no noise, dashes through Gorehorn's shock ring.
  - *Implementation:* tools/balance/pilot.ts turns World state into an Intent every tick. Each tick it builds a danger map over 16 headings and 3 look-ahead times (0.15 / 0.3 / 0.5 s), scoring straight-line-extrapolated hostile projectiles, TELEGRAPH/SHOCK hazards whose remaining life is shorter than the look-ahead, and enemy contact. It adds desire terms: preferred range (Vanguard 60 px, Weaver 260, Phantom 200), pickups after a clear, and the exit door through a door-seeded BFS. It dashes when the best heading's risk is above a threshold. It only emits Intent, so bots run the same code paths as humans.
  - *Acceptance:* An Expert Vector Vanguard kills Gorehorn in at least 90% of 200 floor-1 seeds. The pilot costs at most 0.05 ms per tick with 50 enemies. Novice < Average < Expert win rates hold for every class.
- **Build and choice policies** — Policies for card picks (random, greedy-by-tag, value from the Epic 3 budget model), stat allocation (Vanguard STR/VIT 2:1, Weaver INT/FOC/VIT, Phantom AGI/FOC), item equip, shop, forge, events, and path choice. Path choice example: head for the shop when holding at least 80 gems, the rest node when HP is under 50%, an elite when HP is over 75%.
  - *Implementation:* tools/balance/policies.ts implements DecisionPolicy variants. A policy's own randomness comes from hashCombine(runSeed, policySalt), never Math.random.
  - *Acceptance:* The same seed and policy always produce the same decisions. The report lists the full policy matrix. With the budget-value policy, average power at the floor-2 boss is at least 15% higher than with the random policy.
- **Telemetry recorder** — Zero-allocation counters inside the sim: damage taken per AttackId, damage dealt per source (basic attack, ability, fork, chain, burn, satellites, scythe, cross, axe, holyWater, requiem, thorns, moonDash), and per-room records (kind, column, ticks, HP in/out, gems by source, kills by enemy type).   - *Implementation:* New src/game/telemetry.ts: SimStats built on fixed typed arrays. World gets stats: SimStats | null. hitEnemy, hurtPlayer, collect, killEnemy and the controller's sinks do a guarded increment. A sourceId parameter is threaded through hitEnemy calls.
  - *Acceptance:* In perf.test, tick time grows by at most 5% with stats on and is unchanged with them off. Totals reconcile: sum of dmgDealtBySource matches player.damageDealt within 0.1%.
- **Batch runner** — A CLI matrix runner, e.g. npm run balance -- --runs 300 --classes all --skills avg,expert --mode hunter --heat 0. Seeds are deterministic, output is JSONL, and runs can be resumed. A run is about 180k ticks; at about 0.06 ms per tick that is about 11 s, so a 450-run smoke matrix takes about 10 min on 8 cores.
  - *Implementation:* tools/balance/run.ts, run with vite-node, fanning out over node worker_threads. Seed i = hashCombine(baseSeed, i). Each record holds seed, class, skill, policy, mode, heat, result, floor reached, death AttackId, room records, level per floor, gem flow, picks and config hash.

- **Balance dashboard** — A single self-contained HTML report. It shows win rate by class x skill x mode x heat with confidence intervals; a survival curve by room index; a Pareto of death causes (e.g. 'Count Vlador / Blood Spiral 31%'); boss TTK histograms against target bands; card pick rate vs win-rate lift; gem sources vs sinks per floor; level-by-floor box plots; and deltas against a baseline.
  - *Implementation:* tools/balance/report.ts writes balance-report/index.html (gitignored, never bundled) with inline SVG charts drawn in code and no libraries.
  - *Acceptance:* Opens offline in Chrome, Firefox and Safari. Every chart shows its target band. Generates in under 5 s for 10k runs.

Gates: Average/Hunter win rate 35-60% per class; class spread at most 12 points; no AttackId causes more than 25% of deaths; median combat room 30-75 s; zero rooms over 180 s; zero telegraph violations.
  - *Implementation:* tests/balance.smoke.test.ts (vitest, 60 runs per class) plus tests/balance/baseline.json with tolerances. The nightly job uses the runner (needs the tooling team's CI).
  - *Acceptance:* Raising Phantom dagger damage from 9 to 14 fails the gate with a readable message naming the metric and the delta.

#### BAL-3 · The Ledger: class, card, item and enemy power budgets

*Game design, balance & difficulty · effort L · impact 5/5*

Put a measurable power price on every class, card, item and monster, so all 3 classes are viable and no single card (such as Reaper's Scythe) decides a run. **Player value:** Every class feels strong in its own way, card picks become real decisions, and builds have identity without automatic wins.

- **Damage and survivability model (EDPS/EHP)** — Pure functions for effective damage per second (EDPS) and effective HP (EHP), in three scenarios: Duel (single boss at 300 px), Pack (8 Skeletons at 120 px) and Swarm (20 Blood Bats). EHP = maxHp x (100+armor)/100 + regen x 60 s + lifesteal x EDPS x 60 s. A card's or item's value is 0.6 x its EDPS gain % + 0.4 x its EHP gain %, measured against a per-class 'floor-2 reference build' (level 5, 3 commons, weapon forged to level 2).
  - *Implementation:* New src/game/balance/budget.ts, which imports cards.ts, equipment.ts, classes.ts and stats.ts deriveStats. Calibrate against Epic 2's per-source DPS measurements.
  - *Acceptance:* Model predictions are within +/-15% of bot-measured DPS for the basic attack and the 6 sub-weapon/familiar hooks, for all 3 classes.
- **Class normalization pass** — Close the level-1 gap (Phantom about 94 DPS vs Vanguard 58 vs Weaver 48). Starting values: Phantom daggers 9 to 7 damage and 0.26 to 0.29 s (about 69 DPS with crit, still the best single target, but it has to aim); Weaver orbs 13 to 15 damage and 0.34 to 0.32 s (about 58 DPS), HP 70 to 85, armor 2 to 4, Singularity Pulse cooldown 6 to 5.5 s. Vanguard is unchanged; arc cleave is its edge against packs.
  - *Implementation:* Data edits in classes.ts, recorded in the budget model. Card text is generated from data, so it updates with the numbers.
  - *Acceptance:* Level-1 Duel EDPS spread across classes is at most 20% in the model. Epic 2 Average/Hunter class win rates are within 8 points of each other.
- **Card rescoring by rarity band** — Value bands against the reference build: common 6-10%, uncommon 10-15%, rare 15-22%, epic 22-32%. Proposed fixes: Forked Vector to 2 shards at 30%, at most once per attack volley. Reaper's Scythe to 1.8x base with +25% crit chance instead of always critting. Requiem to 3x base within 640 px. Split Volley gets a per-class multiplier (Weaver +1 orb at 70% damage). Burning Hex becomes 20% of base damage per second per stack (max 3 stacks per enemy). Phase Satellites becomes 4 + 35% of base. Iron Will becomes +30 HP and +5% HP.
  - *Implementation:* Add value-relevant fields to CardDef in cards.ts (perClass multipliers, maxStacksPerTarget). Update the hook math in playerCombat.ts and world.ts (forkAt, the burn block in hitEnemy, updateFamiliars, the requiem loop).
  - *Acceptance:* New tests/budget.test.ts checks every card x class against its rarity band, or requires an explicit budgetOverride with a written reason.
- **Stack rules and caps** — Cards declare maxStacks: Forked Vector 2, Reaper's Scythe 2, Requiem 3, Singularity Core 1, Vampiric Fang 3, Thorned Mail 3. Stacking is linear or diminishing (x0.7 per extra stack). Lifesteal is capped at 8% globally.
  - *Implementation:* cards.ts: maxStacks and stackCurve fields. offerCards filters maxed cards. player.recompute applies the curve. CONFIG.caps.lifesteal = 0.08, enforced in deriveStats.
  - *Acceptance:* Across 10k seeded offers, offerCards never shows a maxed card. With every card stacked 5 times, deriveStats gives lifesteal of at most 0.08.
- **Card-offer quality and RNG hygiene** — Fix repeated offers by salting card RNG with the room seed, interactable index and an offer counter. Add tag pity: if the player owns at least 3 stacks of a tag and the last 2 offers had none of it, the next offer guarantees one. Add a 'Holy Candle' reroll token, one per rest node.
  - *Implementation:* app.ts / runController cardRng gains the salts. offerCards gets a pity parameter. Candle count is stored in PlayerSave. All draws stay on STREAM.cards.
  - *Acceptance:* Across 10k seeded runs, no two offers in a run are identical. The tag-pity unit test passes. The same seed with the same rerolls gives the same cards.
- **Item and forge budgets** — Express all 21 items in budget points (1 BP = +1% value on the reference build). Targets: common 4, uncommon 7, rare 11, epic 16 BP, +/-20%. Examples to check: Grim Sickle (epic, +11 dmg, +5% crit) vs Bloodforged Claymore (rare, +9 dmg, +1% lifesteal) vs Imp Bell (epic, 2 familiars x 12 dmg). Forge value should be about 1 BP per 8 gems at every level.
  - *Implementation:* Data edits in equipment.ts, plus budget.ts itemValue().
  - *Acceptance:* The budget test lists every item inside its band, and forge value per gem stays within band for levels 1-6.
- **Enemy threat ledger** — For each enemy type, threat = effective HP x damage potential (contact damage x a reach factor for its AI, plus shotDamage / fireEvery x bot-measured hit rate). Suggested spawn cost = k x sqrt(threat), compared with current costs (Blood Bat 1, Skeleton 2, Ghoul 3, Bone Archer 3, Hex Witch 4, Gargoyle 5, Axe Armor 6) and with bot-measured 'player HP lost per enemy' and 'seconds to kill' per floor.
  - *Implementation:* budget.ts enemyThreat() combined with Epic 2 telemetry (kills by type, damage by AttackId). The dashboard prints suggested vs current cost, and enemyDefs.ts costs are updated from it.
  - *Acceptance:* Every enemy type's cost/threat ratio is within +/-25% of the median, shown in a dashboard table.

**Depends on:** needs Epic 2 (Bloodhound) for calibration and verification; needs UI: card and item text generated from data so changed numbers show up automatically; needs the content team: new cards, items and enemies must declare budget values to pass CI
**Risks:** Nerfing Phantom and Reaper's Scythe can feel bad to existing players; announce through the balance changelog (Epic 8) and buff the losers rather than only nerfing the winners; Normalizing too hard makes builds samey; bands allow 1.5x spread inside a rarity; The model can drift from real play; keep calibration against bots in CI
