#### BIO-8 · 8. Biome wave B: Royal Chapel, Alchemy Lab, Colosseum

*Biomes, run structure & world · effort L · impact 4/5*

Fill out the castle with a stained-glass cathedral of bells and holy light, a bubbling lab of acid and chain-reacting flasks, and a blood-sand Colosseum gauntlet where the crowd's Hype meter decides your rewards. **Player value:** Each tier gains a third fork option, Long runs get their Gauntlet act, and the art direction gets three of its most striking spaces: stained glass, neon-green alchemy and a sunlit arena.

- **The Royal Chapel ('Hymns for no one')** — Tier 3. Palette: ivory 0xefe6d2, gold, cathedral red carpet and jewel-tone glass (sapphire 0x3a5bd8, ruby, emerald). Layout: a symmetric cross of nave and transept with rows of pews as low walls (shots fly over, feet don't), and side chapels in the BSP leaves. Hazards: LIGHT_BEAMs from stained glass, BELL_TOLL rings and HOLY_GROUND around altars. Decor: pews, a rose window, organ pipes, angel statues, censers and candle racks. Event 'The Confessional': give up 1 card of your choice for 2 stat points and a full heal. Reward identity: HOLY-tag cards +35% weight and rest heals +10%. Music: an A minor organ chorale in 3/4.
  - *Implementation:* Generation uses symmetry 'mirrorY' and carpet 'nave' (3 tiles wide along the centreline). A `layPews(rect)` pass writes rows of TILE_LOW with a 2-tile centre aisle. The rose window is a 3-tile-wide wall decor drawn with radial segments in one GraphicsContext. Beams use the hazard rule 'nearWindow'. Spawn rows: Cherub Imp (a cute winged chibi), Fallen Paladin (shielded charger), Choir Wraith (casts sonic rings), Skeleton, Gargoyle. Boss: the Hollow Choirmaster, a golem of organ pipes, in the Nave arena.
  - *Acceptance:* Before doors are carved, tiles mirror exactly across the centreline (tiles[y][x] == tiles[h−1−y][x]). Pews never block the safe path. A sim test confirms projectiles cross pews. Beam healing stops at 25% per room.
- **The Alchemy Lab ('Bubbling, boiling, badly labelled')** — Tier 2. Palette: toxic green 0x7cff4f, violet 0x8a4fff, copper 0xc87533 and soot. Layout: a grid of lab rooms with rows of workbenches as low walls, acid vats (acid pools with ink rims) and storage closets. Hazards: barrels and flasks that chain-react (acid flasks leave a 4 s puddle), steam vents, tesla coil pairs, and Transmutation Circles: stand in one for 3 s to turn 20 gems into a random uncommon item, once per room. Decor: alembics, bubbling cauldrons, homunculus jars, flask shelves and chalk diagrams. Event 'The Philosopher's Cauldron': sacrifice an equipped item to roll one of the next rarity up. Reward identity: forge costs −15% and 70% of chests hold items. Music: whole-tone pizzicato with bubbling arpeggios.
  - *Implementation:* Generation uses bspMinLeaf 8 with `splitBias` toward halves (for the grid feel), 3-wide corridors and cavernFraction 0.1. A `layBenches(rect)` pass adds benches, and vats are acid blobs from a small cellular-automaton pass inside rectangles. Add InteractKind 'circle' with a dwell timer. Spawn rows: Homunculus, Mitosis Slime (splits in two on death, up to 2 generations), Flask Imp (throws acid flasks), Ghoul, Hex Witch. Boss: Doctor Paracelsior and the Brass Homunculus, a two-body fight in the Workshop arena.
  - *Acceptance:* 5 barrels in a line all detonate within 1.25 s and damage both teams. The Mitosis Slime makes ≤ 4 descendants per original. Each circle converts once per room, deterministically.
- **The Colosseum gauntlet ('The crowd wants blood')** — Tier 4, used in Long runs and as an optional detour fork. Palette: sunlit sand 0xe8c37a, terracotta 0xb5532e and crimson banners, with a blue-sky void ringed by the crowd. Layout: oval arenas (64x44) with lion gates. Each node is a 3-5 wave gauntlet with a Bounty modifier such as 'Elite Axe Armor', 'Sand Spikes' or 'Night Match' (darkness). The Hype meter fills with stylish play: multi-kills within 1 s, ring-outs, crits and waves cleared without a hit. At 50% the crowd throws hearts; at 100% it throws a rare chest, and the room's chest rarity goes up one step. Each hit you take drains 10%. Hazards: spike plates and harmless thrown tomatoes that slow. Music: C minor brass stabs over timpani.
  - *Implementation:* Uses src/gen/arena.ts (shared with the Tower from Epic 6) plus a Colosseum ring prefab. Hype is a World value fed by WorldEvents through a fixed-size ring buffer of kill timestamps, and crowd throws use world.rng. The crowd is wall-band decor: rows of chunky cartoon heads that bob with one shared sine offset per row. Bounties reuse RoomRule from Epic 4. Spawn rows: Gladiator Skeleton, Retiarius (throws slowing nets), Manticore Cub, Axe Armor. Boss: Champion Bloodhorn, Gorehorn's cousin, on the Arena Sands.
  - *Acceptance:* Sim tests confirm the Hype increments match the spec. Each threshold rewards once per room. The crowd uses ≤ 6 Graphics rows. The Colosseum appears only in Long runs or as a detour fork.
- **Biome elites and named mini-bosses** — Each wave B biome gets 2 named mini-bosses for the Mini-boss Halls: 'Sister Cymbal, the Bell Wraith', 'Mr. Bubbles, Elder Slime', 'Brutus the Netless' and others. Each also gets elite pools that favour its signature enemy.
  - *Implementation:* BiomeDef.miniBosses is `{base, name, hpMul, modifier}[]`, picked with STREAM.enemies. The name shows in the existing HUD boss bar.
  - *Acceptance:* Each biome's Mini-boss Hall spawns a mini-boss from its list, and the name appears in the boss bar.
- **Biome merchants and events** — Each merchant gets a costume and a stock tilt. In the Chapel, 'Brother Tallow' sells holy cards and potions. In the Lab, 'Professor Fizzwick' sells items and forge discounts. In the Colosseum, 'The Bookie' takes bets of 30-90 gems on the next gauntlet, paying 1.8× for a clear without being hit. Each biome also gets 2 new events.
  - *Implementation:* economy.ts changes to `shopStock(seed, ctx)` reading `biome.shop: {cardTags?, itemShare, potionPrice, special?: 'bet'}`. A bet is a new ShopEntry kind, resolved on the World's 'cleared' event using a no-hit flag.
  - *Acceptance:* The Chapel shop offers at least one HOLY card in ≥ 90% of 500 seeds. A won bet pays exactly 1.8× the stake. Event pools are filtered by biome.

**Depends on:** EPICS 1-4 and EPIC 6 (the shared arena generator); ENEMY: Cherub Imp, Fallen Paladin, Choir Wraith, Homunculus, Mitosis Slime, Flask Imp, Gladiator Skeleton, Retiarius, Manticore Cub; BOSS: Hollow Choirmaster, Doctor Paracelsior, Champion Bloodhorn; ART: stained glass, lab props, crowd, merchant costumes; AUD: organ voice, pizzicato, noise-based crowd roar; ECON/CARD: HOLY card weighting, bets
**Risks:** Chapel imagery: keep it gothic fantasy (bells, light, organ) and avoid real religious figures.; Chain reactions can trigger huge particle bursts. Cap explosion particles per frame.; Hype may reward one playstyle over others. Tune its thresholds per class with autoplay simulations.; A bright sunlit arena must still read with dark ink outlines and bloom.
**Constraint check:** Uses the same pipeline as wave A: vector art, synthesized audio and seeded streams. The crowd's per-row sine bob and the fixed Hype ring buffer keep the hot loop allocation-free. Bundle grows about 30 KB gzipped. Across all epics, the total grows from about 250 KB to about 400-450 KB gzipped, well under the 1 MB budget.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (revise, effort XL): Nine enemies, three bosses, crowd audio, the Hype system and art for three biomes is XL, not L. The Colosseum overlaps the Tower of Trials and GEN-7 Trials; reuse one arena loop.
- *player* (cut, effort XL): Biomes four to six before the first new one ships is premature, and the Colosseum duplicates Tower of Trials. Defer to post-launch; three biomes at L is underestimated.
- *completeness* (keep): Fine later content once wave A proves the pipeline. Chapel, priest and holy-light imagery needs an age-rating and culturalization check, not just avoiding real figures.

#### GEN-7 · Trials and Riddles: challenge and puzzle rooms

*Procedural generation technology · effort L · impact 4/5*

Two optional node types. Trials are timed, rule-bound combat challenges started by ringing a Bell of Trials. Riddles are puzzles generated solvable by construction: coffin Sokoban, brazier sequences, moonbeam mirrors and organ melodies, each with a verifier in the test suite. **Player value:** A change of pace between fights. Skill trials pay in epic chests, riddles reward thinking, and neither can ever block a run.

- **New node types and graph rules** — Add challenge and puzzle nodes to the floor graph with caps, column rules, unlocked exits and map badges.
  - *Implementation:* NodeType gains 'challenge' | 'puzzle'. CONFIG.nodeWeights rebalances to combat 44, elite 12, event 12, shop 10, rest 14, challenge 5, puzzle 4. Caps: puzzle at most 1 per floor, challenge at most 2. Neither appears in column 1 or the pre-boss column. World.loadRoom keeps doors unlocked for both. NODE_LABEL gets 'TRIAL' and 'RIDDLE' and new badge icons (UI).
  - *Acceptance:* 10,000 graphs pass all old and new invariants. Doors are unlocked on entry in both node types (sim test).
- **Challenge framework** — A state machine around an opt-in bell: countdown, timer, success or fail, and tiered rewards.
  - *Implementation:* src/game/challenges.ts plus CHALLENGE_DEFS data. Flow: Bell of Trials interactable → 3 s countdown → active → success or fail → reward altar. Tiers gold, silver and bronze map to epic, rare and common chests. Layout comes from 'Trial Arena' set-piece variants; the composition comes from passRng(CHALLENGE). Timers run on sim time. The HUD gets a timer pill (UI).
  - *Acceptance:* The spawn timeline is deterministic per seed. Rewards are granted once (RoomDelta.solved). Walking out mid-trial forfeits cleanly with no lock-in.
- **Trial roster** — Six launch trials using traps, destructibles and the existing enemy roster.
  - *Implementation:* Gauntlet of the Pendulum: cross a 30-tile Pendulum Gallery to the far bell in at most 20 s, taking at most 2 hits. Bat Swarm Siege: kill 40 + 10×floor Blood Bats in 40 s, in flocks of 8 from roost sockets. Untouchable Crypt: clear 3 waves without damage for an epic chest; otherwise a common one. Duel of the Armors: 2 elite Axe Armors in a 14x10 ring with spike strips, 60 s. Candle Sprint: break 20 candles in 25 s. Blood Moon Vigil: survive 45 s with healing disabled while the enemy budget ramps from 1.0× to 2.0×. All numbers live in CONFIG.challenges.
  - *Acceptance:* A scripted headless bot completes Candle Sprint and Bat Swarm Siege on 50 seeds in sim tests. Every trial's targets are readable from CONFIG, and the failure states never softlock.
- **Puzzle generators built by reverse construction** — Every puzzle is generated from its solved state and scrambled with legal reverse moves, so it is solvable by construction.
  - *Implementation:* src/gen/puzzles/. Coffin Sokoban (Catacombs): a 9x7 region with 2–3 coffins on pressure plates, then 12–30 reverse pulls (the reverse of a push) from the player's position. Brazier Order (Castle Gate): 4–5 braziers whose order is shown in stained-glass colours. Moonbeam Mirrors (Throne): 3–4 mirrors with 4 orientations each; the generator picks the solution and scrambles it, aiming at least 3 rotations away. Organ Melody (Throne): floor keys repeat a 4–6 note phrase played by a ghost organist, using the procedural music synth (AUD). Difficulty is estimated from reverse-move count, so no solver ships in the bundle.
  - *Acceptance:* Each type generates in at most 3 ms. Over 10,000 seeds, the solution length is non-trivial: Sokoban needs at least 6 pushes, Mirrors at least 3 rotations.
- **Puzzle entities and reset** — Input-agnostic puzzle pieces with tile-snapped pushing, rotation, lighting and a reset lever.
  - *Implementation:* A PuzzleStore SoA holds pushables (snap to tiles; a push starts after 0.2 s of moving into the block, which works with keyboard, stick and touch joystick), mirrors (Interact rotates 90°), braziers (lit by any player hit; a wrong order puffs 5 damage and resets) and organ keys (step on them). Pushes call setTile, so flow fields and the minimap update. The Reset Lever restores the generated state. Puzzles reset on reload; only the solved flag persists.
  - *Acceptance:* Pushing is driven purely by Intent, with no device branches. A fuzz of 1,000 random push sequences shows no overlap or clipping. Reset restores the exact initial state hash.
- **Solvability verifiers and an accessibility skip** — Test-only solvers prove every generated puzzle can be solved, and a brute-force skip guarantees nobody is ever stuck.
  - *Implementation:* tests/puzzles.test.ts holds a BFS Sokoban solver (state = normalised player region plus coffin set, capped at 200,000 states), plus exhaustive solvers for mirrors (4^k ≤ 256 states) and brazier order. Difficulty bands: Sokoban 8–25 pushes on floor 1, Mirrors 3–6 rotations. The reward is a Riddle Chest (rare or better, plus 1 extra card choice). The Brute Force option smashes the reward cage at a cost of 20% max HP.
  - *Acceptance:* 10,000 generated puzzles of each type are 100% solvable within their difficulty bands. The cage-smash path always grants the reward minus one rarity tier.

**Depends on:** Epic 1 (setTile, RoomDelta.solved, pass streams); Epic 3 (Trial Arena and riddle set-pieces); Epic 4 (Candle Sprint destructibles); Epic 5 (Pendulum Gallery, spike strips); UI: TRIAL and RIDDLE badges, timer pill, puzzle prompts; AUD: organ melody phrases via the procedural music engine; ART: coffin push block, mirrors, braziers, organ keys, Bell of Trials
**Risks:** Sokoban on touch can feel fiddly. Use generous snap timing and highlight the push direction.; Organ Melody depends on audio, so it needs a visual fallback (keys glow) for muted or deaf players.; Taking node weight from combat lowers XP per floor. Rebalance with ECO.
**Constraint check:** Generators run once at room load in at most 3 ms. Solvers live only in tests, so there is zero bundle or runtime cost. Puzzle pieces are SoA and Intent-driven, so they work identically on touch, keyboard and mouse, and gamepad. All randomness comes from pass streams. Visuals are vector, and the organ uses the existing synthesizer with no samples.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (revise, effort XL): Absorb BIO-7's biome puzzles so there is one puzzle framework. Sokoban blocks need a new sim entity and touch snapping; organ melodies need a visual fallback. Four verified generators is XL.
- *player* (revise): Bell Trials fit the combat loop; keep them. Sokoban and organ-melody puzzles stall an action roguelite and are fiddly on touch; limit to one puzzle type, owned by BIO-7.
- *completeness* (keep): Good optional content. Ship Trials plus two puzzle types first. Organ Melody needs Bluetooth latency tolerance as well as the visual fallback.

#### BLD-7 · E7 · Relics, Curses & Blessings

*Combat systems & buildcraft · effort L · impact 4/5*

Passive run relics with real drawbacks, boss signature relics, a Curse Level that trades danger for loot luck, curses that turn into blessings when you meet their objective, cursed chests, and an Exorcist's lectern. It also fixes today's non-persistent curse bug. **Player value:** Gothic bargains on every floor: wear the Glass Rosary for +40% damage knowing every hit costs hearts, or take the Curse of the Bat, kill 40 bats and walk away blessed with a bat familiar.

- **Relic inventory & data model** — Relics are passive run items, each with a boon and a bane. You can hold any number, but only one copy of each. They show as a row of little vector trinkets in the pause Equipment tab and as a compact strip under the gem pill. Sources:
- Elite chests: 35% chance of a relic instead of an item.
- Boss reliquary: pick 1 of 3, always including the boss's signature relic.
- Shop: 1 relic slot, 90–160 gems.
- Events.
  - *Implementation:* src/game/relics.ts holds RELICS {id, name, rarity, glyph, boon: Partial<Mods> | effect, bane: Partial<Mods> | effect, desc, sig?: bossKey}. Player.relics: string[] is folded into recompute (mods) and the effect registry (behaviours). PlayerSave stores relics. spawnBossRewards in world.ts adds a reliquary interactable (dependency LEVEL/ART prop).
  - *Acceptance:* Relics survive a save/load round-trip. A mods test confirms that both boon and bane apply. The boss reliquary always includes the floor boss's signature relic.
- **Relics with teeth (28, incl. boss signatures)** — Examples:
- Mirror of Twin Moons: sub-weapons fire twice; −25% max HP.
- Glass Rosary: +40% damage; each hit taken also costs 3 hearts.
- Gravedigger's Spade: +1 gem per kill; −10% move.
- Stopped Pocket-Watch: each room opens with a free 2 s Stopwatch; −15% CDR.
- Leech Crown: +6% life steal; campfires and Pot Roast heal 50% less.
- Bell of Vespers: every 30 s, stuns all enemies for 1 s and gives +1 heart; you are rooted for 0.3 s.
- Blood-Soaked Tarot: 4 card choices; rerolls cost double.
- Cracked Hourglass: +25% attack speed; dash cooldown +40%.
- Silver Nail: HOLY ignores 50% resist; BLOOD −30%.
- Witch's Thimble: statuses you apply last +50%, and so do statuses on you.
- Iron Boots of the Penitent: immune to knockback and chill; −15% move.
- Vampire's Signet: BLOOD +30%; hearts drop 50% less.
- Lantern of the Lost: reveals every node type on the map; card offers show only 2 cards.
- Executioner's Hood: +60% crit damage; non-crits −15%.
Boss signatures:
- Gorehorn's Twin Tusk: dashes end in a mini-slam (80% PHYS, radius 90); dash cooldown +0.2 s.
- Colossus Rune-Heart: every 8 s, emits 12 rune bolts at 70% ARCANE; −10% move.
- Vlador's Cape: dash becomes a bat-swarm blink that passes through enemies and applies 1 bleed; HOLY −25%.
  - *Implementation:* Data plus about 14 effect modules. Drawbacks are negative mods or effect parameters. Stat floors (max HP ≥ 20, move ≥ 60% of base) live in deriveStats.
  - *Acceptance:* Every relic has a test asserting both clauses. The balance harness includes relic-heavy builds. No drawback can push a stat below its floor.
- **Curse system & Curse Level** — Curses are run modifiers that bite now and pay off later. Curse Level, the sum of curse severities from 0 to 10, adds per level: enemy HP +6%, elite chance +3%, loot luck +0.02 (finally driving rollRarity's unused luck parameter) and souls +5%. The curses:
- Curse of the Bat: Blood Bats +50% speed and ×2 spawn weight.
- Curse of Brittle Bones: +15% PHYS damage taken.
- Curse of the Red Moon: every 20 s, a blood pulse costs 10% of current HP.
- Hexed Purse: −30% gems.
- Witch's Malady: statuses on you last +50%.
- Pallid Heart: no natural regen.
- Doomed Hunter: enemy projectiles +20% speed.
  - *Implementation:* src/game/curses.ts holds CURSES {id, severity, mods/effects, lift}. Curse state lives on Run and is saved in RunSave v2. World.loadRoom passes curse modifiers {hpMul, eliteChance, weightMul} to planSpawns using the enemies stream, never the layout stream (dependency GEN). rollRoomLoot receives luck.
  - *Acceptance:* At Curse Level 5, spawned enemy HP is ×1.30 and the elite rate is +15 points. The rarity distribution shifts (100k-sample test). Floor layouts are byte-identical with and without curses. Curses persist through Continue Run.
- **Lifting curses into blessings** — Every curse has an objective that turns it into a matching blessing. Progress shows as a ring on the curse icon.
- Kill 40 Blood Bats → Blessing of the Bat: +1 bat familiar, bats drop ×2 hearts.
- Kill 30 skeletons → Ossuary Blessing: +10 armor.
- Clear 3 rooms losing under 20% HP each → Blessing of the Blood Moon: +15% BLOOD.
- Spend 150 gems → Merchant's Favour: shop prices −20%.
- Cleanse 5 statuses → Witchward: burn immunity.
- Skip one campfire heal → Pallid Vigor: +2 HP/s regen.
- 15 perfect dodges → Moonlit Grace: perfect window +50%.
  - *Implementation:* Objectives subscribe to effect-registry triggers: onKill with a def filter, onRoomClear, onPerfectDodge, onStatusCleansed. Counters live in a Uint16Array. Conversion pushes a WorldEvent 'curseLifted' for the banner.
  - *Acceptance:* Each curse has an objective test. Conversion removes the curse's severity from Curse Level. Conversion is driven by counters only, so it is deterministic.
- **Blessings & sacred sources** — Positive modifiers, many lasting a set number of rooms:
- Blessing of St. Brigid: negate the first hit in each of the next 3 rooms.
- Fountain's Vigor: +20% move for 2 rooms.
- Candlelit Vigil: +1 heart per kill for 1 room.
Permanent blessings come from lifted curses. Sources include the Moonlit Fountain event, S-rank rooms (E4) and the Exorcist's lectern.
  - *Implementation:* Same data model as curses, plus roomsLeft, which decrements on enterRoom. St. Brigid's shield is an onHurt effect that zeroes damage.
  - *Acceptance:* Room-count expiry test passes. St. Brigid negates exactly one hit per room and pops a gold halo.
- **Cursed chests, Exorcist lectern & voluntary pacts** — - Cursed chests: chained purple chests in 8% of combat rooms on floors 2–3 give an epic relic plus a random curse.
- Exorcist's lectern: found in Sanctuaries, it removes one curse for 50 gems or 10 hearts.
- Pact of the Night: an altar at the start of each floor lets you take a curse in exchange for an extra relic choice.
  - *Implementation:* InteractKind gains cursedChest, exorcist and pact, handled in world.ts and in app.ts onInteract. Spawns roll on the loot stream. Props come from LEVEL/ART.
  - *Acceptance:* Over 1,000 seeds, cursed chest frequency lands within ±1.5 points of target. Each interaction applies the correct state. Every choice screen works with keyboard, gamepad and touch.
- **Persistence fix & save v2** — Fix the Forgotten Armory bug: the −10 max HP is applied through p.metaAttrs.vit, which is not saved, so it vanishes on Continue Run. It becomes a proper 'Chained Relic' curse stored in the run save. RunSave and PlayerSave move to version 2, carrying relics, curses, blessings, hearts, sub-weapon, ledger and banish list.
  - *Implementation:* run.ts gets RunSave version 2 with migrate(v1) filling defaults. app.ts applyEvent cursedItem calls addCurse('chained_relic') and no longer mutates metaAttrs directly.
  - *Acceptance:* Regression test: take the cursed armory, save, load with Player.fromSave, and max HP is still −10 (this fails on current code). v1 fixture saves load into v2 with defaults.

**Depends on:** GEN/spawner: accept curse modifiers (HP multiplier, elite chance, spawn weight) via the enemies stream; LEVEL/ART: cursed chest, Exorcist lectern, Pact altar and reliquary props; EVENTS domain: new events (Reliquary, Pact of the Night); SAVE: v2 migration; META: relic codex and discovery; E1 effect registry, E3 hearts, E4 perfect-dodge trigger
**Risks:** Stacked drawbacks can create unwinnable runs. Stat floors and the Exorcist provide an out.; Curse modifiers must never reshape map layouts. They apply only to enemy stats and weights on the enemies stream, and a layout-identity test guards it.; Many relic icons clutter phone screens. On narrow screens they collapse into a 'Relics ×N' chip that opens a sheet.
**Constraint check:** Relics, curses and blessings are data plus effect modules folded at recompute. Per-tick cost is O(1) timers only (Bell of Vespers, Red Moon). Trinket glyphs join the inline SVG icon set, with no raster assets. Every roll stays on seeded streams (loot, events, shop, enemies), and layouts are untouched. About 10 KB gzipped. Saves stay offline in IndexedDB with versioned migration.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (revise): Drop Curse Level (MET-4 owns heat). 'Relic' collides with BIO-7's Count's Relics and MET-3's heirlooms; rename one. The curse-persistence fix belongs in TEC-4's RunSave v2.
- *player* (revise): Relics with drawbacks are good. Curse Level duplicates MET-4 heat; merge it there. Ship the curse-persistence bug fix now as a standalone patch, not inside an L epic.
- *completeness* (revise): Relics are good. Curse Level duplicates MET-4, BAL-7 and SOC-3 heat. 'Relic' also names BIO-7's Count's Relics and CLS-6 heirlooms, so settle terms.

#### BLD-8 · E8 · The Arsenal: Weapon Families, Affixes and Swapping

*Combat systems & buildcraft · effort XL · impact 4/5*

Weapons that change how you attack: ten attack patterns across class-affinity families, elemental affixes, a two-weapon loadout with a Swap Strike, and charged heavy attacks. This includes the Castlevania chain-whip Morning Star. **Player value:** A Vanguard can trade the greatsword for a lashing Morning Star, a Weaver can channel a storm grimoire, and a Phantom can bank a chakram off walls. Swapping a Frostbitten Halberd into a Blazing whip sets off an instant Steam Burst.

- **WeaponDef: families, patterns & affinity** — Each weapon item belongs to a family with a pattern, base damage, interval, range, type and proc coefficient. The class still supplies stats and its ability. Affinity families:
- Vanguard: Greatsword, Morning Star, Halberd.
- Weaver: Moon Staff, Grimoire of Storms, Censer.
- Phantom: Twin Daggers, Chakram, Silver Crossbow.
- Scythe: a cross-class epic.
Off-affinity weapons work at −15% attack speed. Existing items migrate: Crusader Blade and Bloodforged Claymore become Greatswords, Moonstone Staff a Moon Staff, Silver Kris and Raven Fang Twin Daggers, Holy Morning Star a Morning Star, and Grim Sickle a Scythe.
  - *Implementation:* ItemDef in equipment.ts gains weapon?: WeaponSpec {family, pattern, damage, interval, range, speed, type, procCoef, heavy}. ClassDef.attack becomes the fallback. basicAttack dispatches through a PATTERNS[pattern](w, ax, ay, spec) table in src/game/weapons/{slash, whip, thrust, orb, beam, censer, dagger, chakram, crossbow, reap}.ts, replacing the if/else on atk.kind. The HUD sword pill reads from the spec.
  - *Acceptance:* Golden parity: starter weapons reproduce today's per-class damage and timing exactly. Every weapon item has a valid spec. The sim smoke test passes for every family.
- **Ten attack patterns** — - Greatsword: 110° arc, range 78, every 0.46 s, 22 PHYS.
- Morning Star (chain whip): a line lash 150 long and 16 wide, every 0.55 s, 26 PHYS. Hits in the last 25% of the line (the tip) get +50% and count as HEAVY. The lash cuts candles and blockable projectiles along the line.
- Halberd: a 120 thrust that pierces everything, plus a 270° sweep every 3rd attack; every 0.6 s, 30 PHYS, HEAVY.
- Moon Staff: homing orbs, 13 ARCANE every 0.34 s.
- Grimoire of Storms: hold to channel a 260-long beam that ticks 10 times per second for 30% SHOCK and drains 6 energy per second; releasing discharges a forked bolt.
- Censer: a swinging censer that pulses a ring of radius 120 every 0.7 s for 70% FIRE plus burn.
- Twin Daggers: 2 × 9 PHYS every 0.26 s.
- Chakram: bounces 3 times between enemies and walls, then returns; every 0.5 s, 18 PHYS.
- Silver Crossbow: a heavy bolt every 0.8 s for 45 (50% HOLY), pierces everything.
- Scythe: a 200° reap at range 96, every 0.7 s, 34 BLOOD; crits heal 1%.
  - *Implementation:* Each pattern is 40–120 lines. The whip uses a capsule test against a spatial-hash query, and the beam uses a segment-versus-circle test evaluated at 10 Hz. The chakram uses PF_BOUNCE from E3. All math is on scalars with no allocation.
  - *Acceptance:* Canonical layout tests, e.g. 5 skeletons in a line: the halberd thrust hits all 5 and the greatsword at most 3. The whip tip bonus applies only beyond 75% of its length. Every family's DPS lands inside the balance-harness band.
- **Elemental affixes** — Weapon drops roll 0–2 affixes, by rarity: common 0, uncommon 1, rare 1–2, epic 2.
- Prefixes: Blazing (30% FIRE conversion, 25% burn), Frostbitten (25% chill), Galvanic (20% shock), Serrated (30% bleed), Venomous (35% chance of 2 poison), Hallowed (40% HOLY conversion), Hexing (8% curse).

#### TEC-5 · 5. Stained-Glass Pipeline: baked vector atlases, instanced actors and WebGPU

*Engineering, performance, platform & tooling · effort L · impact 4/5*

The art stays 100% vector, but the GPU should not tessellate and batch thousands of vector instances every frame. Bake each shared GraphicsContext once into a runtime atlas at device resolution, then draw the horde as instanced quads. The goal is 400 Blood Bats, 8,000 Ultra particles and bloom at 120 FPS in 4K, and the Bone Catacombs at 60 FPS on a mid-range phone. **Player value:** Bigger hordes and boss bullet patterns without slowdown. Phones stay smooth and cool for longer. There is headroom for the limb animation, pets and new floors other domains are planning.

- **Runtime vector baker (render/bake.ts)** — Rasterize shared GraphicsContexts into an atlas at runtime. This is still 100% code: textures are generated at runtime and no image files exist.
  - *Implementation:* At boot, and on preset or DPR change (not on dynamic-resolution steps), render the 3 heroes and weapons, 7 enemy bodies plus their 'w' silhouettes, bat wings, the elite aura ring, 12 projectile kinds, 3 pickups, 4 chest rarities x open/closed, props and shadows into one RenderTexture using shelf packing. bakeScale = zoom x min(dpr, dprCap) x renderScaleMax: about 5 px per world unit at 4K Ultra and about 1.5 on a 720p phone at Medium. Atlas cap is 2048² on Ultra (16 MB) and 1024² on Medium/Low. Mipmaps are on so dynres down-steps stay smooth. MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE is respected. Bosses (Gorehorn at 175 units tall, the Rune Colossus, Count Vlador) are single instances and stay vector, crisp at 4K.
  - *Acceptance:* Baking takes under 60 ms on desktop and under 150 ms on Pixel 7 emulation, hidden behind the title fade. Golden diffs between baked and vector rendering are under 1% of pixels at 1080p and 4K. The atlas is viewable in the dev overlay.
- **Instanced actor layer** — Replace per-enemy Container trees with SoA-fed ParticleContainers.
  - *Implementation:* Three ParticleContainers (shadows; bodies + flash silhouettes + bat wings; HP bars) are filled from the EnemyStore each frame. Y-sorting uses a preallocated Int32 index buffer and insertion sort, which is close to O(n) because order changes little between frames. Squash, bob, facing, hit flash and Axe Armor windup glow use the same formulas as syncEnemies today. This removes the per-view new GraphicsContext() for elite auras and the destroy/recreate when a slot changes def.
  - *Acceptance:* In a heavy room (300 enemies, Ultra) the actor layer uses at most 6 draw calls, and CPU frame preparation at 4K in Chrome is at most 2.5 ms at p95. No Graphics or Container is created after room entry (dev counter asserts 0). Goldens match.
- **Projectiles, pickups and hazards on atlas particles** — Stop allocating a Graphics per pool slot for bullets, soul orbs, gems and telegraphs.
  - *Implementation:* projViews, pickupViews and hazardViews (one Graphics or Container per slot today) become rotation-enabled particle batches from the baked atlas. Telegraph discs and rings use baked unit textures scaled per hazard. Holy water fire and the Singularity well use baked radial textures with additive tint.
  - *Acceptance:* Count Vlador's blood spiral with 600 enemy projectiles adds at most 2 draw calls. Rune Colossus ground-spike telegraphs render identically (golden).
- **Static room layers: measure, then chunk-bake on mobile** — Remove the room-entry tessellation hitch and keep per-frame cost low on phones.

On Medium and Low, those layers are baked into 1024² chunk RenderTextures at preset resolution: about 22 MB for a 1,920x1,280-unit combat hall at 1.5 px per unit. An LRU evicts chunks under memory pressure. Torch flames, candelabras, moonbeams and door glows stay live.
  - *Acceptance:* Room-enter main-thread work, measured with performance.measure, is at most 16 ms on desktop and at most 50 ms on a mid phone. No seams at chunk edges in goldens at zoom 1.0 and 1.37.
- **WebGPU path with probe and A/B selection** — Use WebGPU where it actually wins, and keep WebGL2 and Canvas fallbacks.
  - *Implementation:* platform/gpuProbe.ts calls navigator.gpu.requestAdapter() and reads features, limits and adapter.info. The renderer preference becomes ['webgpu','webgl','canvas'] only where the probe passes and a 1.5 s A/B micro-bench (bats400 CPU+GPU frame ms) favours WebGPU by at least 10%. The result is cached per device in settings, with a ?renderer=webgpu|webgl|canvas override. #### SOC-6 · Blood Pact Duo: local co-op

#### SOC-8 · Grimoire Packs: JSON content mods

*Social, multiplayer & community · effort XL · impact 4/5*

Let the community write new nightmares: a Frost Crypt floor theme, a 'Bride of Vlador' boss with her own pattern table, a Gambler's Deck of cursed cards. All of it is plain JSON, validated at load, drawn by the same ink-and-cel pen as the core art, and synthesized by the same ZzFX engine. No scripts, no images, no risk. **Player value:** A long tail of content and community identity at no art or audio cost, while keeping replays, ghosts and boards honest through content hashes.

- **Content registries from the core data** — Turn the hard-coded const arrays into frozen registries the core pack fills, with no hot-loop cost.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/content/registry.ts: `Registry<T>` with `register(nsId, def)`, `freeze()` and `byIndex[]`. The existing ENEMY_DEFS, CARDS, ITEMS, EVENTS, FLOOR_THEMES and curses become the 'core' pack (values unchanged). Enemy indices stay u8 (≤ 255 including mods, enforced) so `EnemyStore.def` is unchanged. `ENEMY`, `CARD_BY_ID` and `BOSS_FOR_FLOOR` are derived after freeze. The sim reads plain arrays with no Map lookups per tick.
  - *Acceptance:* All existing tests pass on registry-backed data. The perf test is unchanged (±2%). Registering after freeze throws.
- **Pack format and a zero-dependency validator** — A strict, versioned schema with precise errors and hard limits.
  - *Implementation:* pack.json is {id, name, version, author, abi range, requires[], content: {enemies[], bosses[], cards[], items[], events[], themes[], curses[], tomes[], sprites[], sfx[]}}. Add a hand-written validator in src/content/validate.ts that returns `{path, message}[]` (e.g. 'cards[3].mods.crit: must be between 0 and 0.5'). Bounds: enemy hp 1–100000, speed 0–600, radius 6–80, fireEvery 0.2–20. Hooks must be in HookKey, ai in the behaviour library, event effects in the EventEffect union. Ids are namespaced ('frostcrypt:frost_skeleton'). Packs are capped at 256 KB. Strings are capped at 400 chars, any 'http', 'data:' or '<' is rejected, and text renders only via textContent or richText (auditing the 20 `html:` sinks in screens.ts).
  - *Acceptance:* A 1000-case mutation fuzz never throws uncaught and always returns errors. An XSS-attempt pack renders as inert text. Validating a 256 KB pack takes < 20 ms.
- **Behaviour library for modded enemies and bosses** — Mods compose existing, tested AI, so no scripting is needed.
  - *Implementation:* Refactor enemyAi.ts into named behaviours with parameters: chase{speed}, flutter{amp, freq}, ranged{fireEvery, shotKind ∈ PROJ, spread}, caster{orbCount}, charger{windup, chargeSpeed}. Boss patterns become a library: radialBurst{n, speed}, aimedFan{n, arc}, rockfall{n}, groundSpikes{rings}, spiral{arms, turns}, summon{defKey, count}, teleport{minDist}. A boss is a phase table: [{hpBelow: 1.0, weights: {aimedFan: 3, summon: 1}}, {hpBelow: 0.4, enrage: true, weights: {spiral: 4}}]. Dispatch goes through a small switch on u8 behaviour ids stored in EnemyStore, so there are no closures in the hot loop.
  - *Acceptance:* A sample 'Bride of Vlador' boss defined purely in JSON runs all phases in a headless vitest. The core bosses (Gorehorn, Rune Colossus, Count Vlador) re-expressed as phase tables keep their pattern frequencies within ±5%. The tick perf is unchanged.
- **Sigil Ink: a vector sprite DSL** — JSON primitive lists compiled once into GraphicsContexts with the house style of thick ink outlines and a single cel highlight.
  - *Implementation:* Sprite JSON is [{"ellipse":[0,-10,14,12],"fill":"#7ccf4a","ink":3,"hl":true},{"poly":[...]},{"eye":[x,y,r,"wink"]},{"rune":[x,y,r]}] plus parts (body, wing, weapon) for the existing transform animation (bob, squash, weapon rotation). It's compiled by render/pen.ts into shared GraphicsContexts at pack load. Limits: ≤ 200 primitives per sprite and ≤ 64 sprites per pack. There's also a 'reskin' mode: reference a core art key plus a palette map (e.g. skeleton with bone→#9fe0ff = Frost Skeleton; golem rune→#ff4fd8). The ART domain's palette tokens in theme.ts are referenced by name.
  - *Acceptance:* A modded sprite renders with the same outline weight as core art at 4K. Compile time is < 2 ms per sprite. Malformed shapes are rejected by the validator, not by Pixi.
- **Synth-only pack audio** — Packs can add sounds and music moods as numbers only, keeping audio fully synthesized.
  - *Implementation:* `sfx` entries are 20-element ZzFX parameter arrays, range-checked per parameter (volume ≤ 1, length ≤ 2 s), and play through the existing 8-voice mixer with a 30 ms merge window. Music moods are {tempo 60–180, scale, instrument: 'harpsichord'|'organ'|'choir', progression[]} parameters for audio/music.ts.
  - *Acceptance:* Pack SFX play and respect the voice cap. Out-of-range parameters are rejected. No audio files are accepted anywhere.
- **Grimoire Library: mod manager** — Import, enable, order and inspect packs, with safe-mode recovery.
  - *Implementation:* Add S.grimoireScreen: import via file picker, drag-drop, or paste a URL (fetched once and cached in IndexedDB 'packs', so network is needed only to fetch). It provides enable/disable, load order, dependency and conflict display, and a validation report. Registries rebuild only between runs, from the title. `?vanilla=1` boots with all packs disabled, and a crash during pack load auto-disables the last enabled pack on the next boot.
