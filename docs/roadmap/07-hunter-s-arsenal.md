# Hunter's Arsenal (v0.8) — Kits, new hunters, Card System v2, the unlock tree, heat and 30 events

[← Roadmap overview](../ROADMAP.md)


**Goal:** Deepen every run with pluggable hunter kits that add an ultimate charged by a Soul Gauge, three new hunters, an 80-card pool with resonance and offer tools, perfect dodge and parry, a stained-glass unlock tree applied through RunRules, a single heat system with difficulty presets, a canon story bible and 30 branching gothic-comedy events. Card screens also get their tarot polish.

**Demo moment:** As the new whip-wielding hunter you chain a perfect dodge into a parry, fill the Soul Gauge and unleash your ultimate. Then three tarot cards deal and flip, and the HOLY resonance chip lights up as your build comes together.

**Why now:** Kits, cards and heat multiply the balance space, so they come after the TEC-7 bots and BAL-3 budgets and after the content they act on: sub-weapons from v0.5, statuses and affixes from v0.7. MET-4 lands after ENM-2 so affix and heat stacking is defined once. STO-2's ambushes and curses ride ENM-4's director and MET-4's mutators. Keeping CLS-4 talents and BLD-8 weapon swaps out of this milestone holds the decision budget per run.

**Estimate:** ~13 weeks

**Exit criteria:**

- [ ] A new hunter is one data entry, one kit module and one art module. A2 and the ultimate fit the frozen UX-9 action set with no new touch button.
- [ ] Six hunters ship (the original 3 plus wave one: the whip hunter, the Dhampir and the Shield Knight, under canon names), each within ±10% of the mean bot win rate at heat 0. Wave two waits for CLS-2's CompanionStore.
- [ ] The 80-card pool launches with reroll, banish, skip and pity. No card raises bot win rate by more than 8 percentage points. SUMMON cards wait for the minion system.
- [ ] Perfect-dodge and parry windows are defined in ticks, with the device class recorded in the RunHeader. The combo meter is folded into the Soul Gauge, and room grades are cut.
- [ ] A ~40-node unlock tree applies through the RunRules snapshot, and dailies use base kits.
- [ ] 12 heat candles absorb BAL-7's heat and SOC-3's curses, with Squire, Hunter and Nightmare presets. A seed's layout is identical at every heat level, and max-heat tick stays within budget.
- [ ] 30 events are drawn deterministically from per-biome pools, the 2 original event bugs have regression tests, and the story bible is published.
- [ ] Tarot cards deal and flip, counters roll, and a nav stack remembers focus, all with reduced-motion variants.

#### CLS-1 · Hunter Kit Framework: data-driven classes with three ability slots

*Classes, characters & companions · effort L · impact 5/5*

Replace the three hard-wired classes with pluggable kits, so a new hunter is one data entry, one kit module and one art module. Every hunter gets Ability 1, Ability 2 and an Ultimate charged by a Soul Gauge. **Player value:** Each existing class gains a second verb and a big ultimate moment right away. Everything else in this plan depends on it: new classes, talents, loadouts and skins.

- **ClassKit contract & registry** — A `ClassKit` interface replaces the id branches: onPrimary(w, ax, ay), onAbility(w, slot, tx, ty), onHold/onRelease(w, slot, dt), onTick(w, dt), onHit(w, enemy, dmg, crit), onHurt(w, raw) → remaining, onKill(w, enemy) and onProjectileHit(w, proj) → PASS|BLOCK|REFLECT.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/game/kits/types.ts, src/game/kits/{vanguard,weaver,phantom}.ts (port basicAttack's three branches, shockwaveSweep, the well and Shadow Step) and src/game/kits/index.ts with `KITS: Record<string, ClassKit>`. ClassId becomes a string validated against the CLASSES registry, and the attack spec moves into the kit. playerCombat.updatePlayer keeps aim, movement and dash and dispatches to `KITS[p.cls.id]`. World.hitEnemy, hurtPlayer, killEnemy and updateProjectiles (enemy-team branch) call the kit hooks. Each run uses one kit, so the call site stays monomorphic.
  - *Acceptance:* `grep -E "cls\.id ===|attack\.kind ===" src/game src/render` returns nothing. A test registers a dummy kit through data alone and passes the sim smoke test. A parity test plays a 3600-tick scripted intent tape on seed 1234 for each original class and gets identical (kills, damageDealt, player x/y, Σ enemy hp) before and after the refactor.
- **Zero-alloc kit state block** — A fixed per-class scratch block replaces one-off Player fields (abilityDashT, guaranteedCrit) and holds charges, stacks and resources.
  - *Implementation:* Add `Player.kit = new Float32Array(32)` and `Player.kitFlags` (Uint32). Each kit declares named slot constants (S_DASH_T = 0, S_CHARGES = 1…). Add `persistSlots` to the kit so PlayerSave.kitState stores only what survives rooms. Bump the RunSave version, and have an old save default kitState to zeros.
  - *Acceptance:* Player has no class-named fields. perf.test runs 10k ticks per kit with heap growth < 64 KB. A v1 run save from the current build continues mid-run with no errors.
- **Three ability slots + hold/charge input** — Slots are Ability 1 (current), Ability 2 and Ultimate. Each ability declares mode 'tap', 'hold' (guard or channel) or 'charge' (release).
  - *Implementation:* Intent gains `ability2` and `ultimate` (edge) plus `abilityHeld` and `ability2Held` (level). input.ts: keyboard Space/RMB = A1, Q = A2, R = Ult. Gamepad A = A1, LB(4)/LT(6) = A2, Y(3) = Ult (all free in play today). Touch adds a second round button above tAbility and an Ultimate pill in the HUD top bar, outside the thumb zone. ClassDef gains `abilities: [AbilityDef, AbilityDef]` and `ultimate: AbilityDef` with {mode, cost, cooldown, chargeMax, icon}. hud.ts renders two cooldown tiles plus a gauge pill.
  - *Acceptance:* A held intent for 60 ticks yields 60 onHold calls and 1 onRelease. On a 640×360 touch viewport every button is ≥ 44 px (CONFIG.minTouchTarget) and outside the right-stick zone. Switching from keyboard to gamepad mid-room relabels keycaps (Q→LB, R→Y) within one frame.
- **Soul Gauge ultimates for the original three** — A 0–100 gauge fills by +2 per enemy-max-HP of damage dealt, +1.5 per kill, +10 per elite kill and +1.5 per 1% of boss HP. Vanguard 'Crimson Crusade': 4 s of +40% Arc Slash size, a WAVE projectile every swing and ×2 knockback. Weaver 'Lunar Eclipse': three wells in a 140 u triangle that collapse into one 260 r burst for 500% base. Phantom 'Thousand Cuts': 1.2 s untargetable while auto-blinking between up to 8 nearest foes, each hit a guaranteed 180% crit.
  - *Implementation:* Player.gauge is charged in World.hitEnemy and killEnemy. Tunables go in CONFIG.ultimate {perHpEquiv: 2, perKill: 1.5, perElite: 10, perBossPct: 1.5, bossCapPct: 0.08}. Ult code lives in each kit. Thousand Cuts reuses w.safePointToward and the hitStamp dedupe.
  - *Acceptance:* In the bot harness the gauge fills in 45–75 s in Floor 1 combat rooms. An ult can't fire below 100. One ult never deals more than 8% of a boss's max HP. The pill tweens green→gold and shows 'ULT READY' using the existing banner CSS.
- **Presentation registry** — The renderer, HUD and UI read class presentation from data, not id checks.
  - *Implementation:* Add src/render/art/heroes/index.ts with `HERO_ART: Record<string, {draw, weapon, extras?, weaponPivot: [x, y], weaponMode: 'swing'|'thrust'|'upright'|'whip', portraitPose}>`. The Weaver rune ring becomes a generic extra. syncPlayerClass and portrait() build from the table. ABILITY_ICON and statPanel's `pref` move into ClassDef as `icons` and `attrPriority`.
  - *Acceptance:* renderer.ts, hud.ts and screens.ts contain no class id string literals. Portrait canvases for Vanguard, Weaver and Phantom hash identically to the pre-refactor build.
- **Kit RNG stream & sim hygiene** — Kit and pet randomness no longer shifts crit and enemy rolls, and Math.random() is banned from gameplay decisions.
  - *Implementation:* Extend core/rng STREAM with kit: 7, companions: 8, loadout: 9. World.loadRoom creates `w.kitRng = new Rng(hashCombine(room.seed, STREAM.kit))`. Move cosmetic jitter in kits behind FxSink. A new tests/hygiene.test.ts scans src/game and src/gen for `Math.random` outside an allow-list (current fx-only lines).
  - *Acceptance:* Adding a proc that consumes kitRng leaves the first 200 draws of w.rng in a room byte-identical. The hygiene test fails if Math.random() is injected into src/game/kits.
- **Kit balance harness** — A headless bot per kit measures clear time, damage taken and ult uses, so no hunter ships 40% stronger than the rest.
  - *Implementation:* tests/harness/bot.ts is a kite-and-fire bot that emits Intents from Rng(77) and uses abilities on cooldown and ults when full. tests/kits.test.ts runs every kit (and later every loadout) over 20 seeds of Floor 1 and 2 combat rooms, prints a table and asserts bands from CONFIG.balance (median clear time within ±15% of the cross-class median, damage taken within ±25%).
  - *Acceptance:* `vitest run tests/kits.test.ts` takes < 60 s, prints the table, and fails when any kit's primary damage is multiplied by 1.5.

**Depends on:** TEC/CI: run the kit harness in CI (it also runs locally); UI/HUD domain: final styling of the second ability tile and the ult pill; Input/accessibility domain: any key-remapping feature must include the A2 and Ult actions
**Risks:** Regressions in a well-tested core; the parity-hash test guards this; Kit hooks inside hitEnemy (thousands of calls/s); keep them monomorphic and benchmark with perf.test; Three buttons crowd the phone thumb zone; the ult goes on the HUD pill, not by the stick; Ult power creep trivialising bosses (8% boss-HP cap per cast)
**Constraint check:** Pure TypeScript logic with no assets. Kit state is a fixed Float32Array with no per-tick closures or allocation. New randomness comes from STREAM.kit, so runs stay reproducible from runSeed + floor + attempt. About +8 KB gzipped. Adds at most one dispatch per event (well under 0.05 ms per tick). Mapped for KB+M, gamepad and touch with live switching via the existing input.method relabel.

**Review notes:**
- *feasibility* (keep): Foundation. Freeze Intent v2 once to cover A2, Ult, sub-weapon, swap and charge; the touch thumb zone can't fit them all. Kit hooks inside hitEnemy must stay monomorphic.
- *player* (revise): Kit framework is a needed enabler, but A2 plus Ult plus BLD-3 sub-weapon plus Item Crash means two supers and six buttons. Make Item Crash the ult, or drop A2.
- *completeness* (keep): Required foundation for CLS-2 to CLS-8. Before adding A2 and Ult, agree a global touch-button budget with UX-3, BLD-3 and BLD-8.

#### CLS-3 · Six New Hunters

*Classes, characters & companions · effort XL · impact 5/5*

Expand the roster from 3 to 9 with a whip-cracking Vampire Hunter, a mist-walking Dhampir, a reaction-chaining Alchemist, a bell-tolling Battle Priest, a pack-leading Beast Tamer and a parrying Shield Knight. Each is unlocked by a deed done in the castle. **Player value:** Triples replayability with sharply different verbs: whip spacing, mist dodging, element reactions, holy zones, pet command and parry timing. It also delivers the core Castlevania fantasy (whip, sub-weapons, hearts, Stopwatch).

- **Morningstar Hunter (Vampire Hunter, motif: Chain)** — Stats: HP 100, ARM 6, SPD 215. Uses 'Hearts' (0–99, starts at 10) instead of energy. Primary 'Vampire-Bane Whip': a 150 u × 18 u capsule, 26 damage every 0.55 s, hits flyers, and the last 30 u tip deals +50% with a crack SFX. A1 'Sub-Weapon' (tap) throws the equipped sub-weapon: Axe (1 heart, high arc, 300%), Cross (1, boomerang), Holy Water (1, lobbed fire), Dagger (½, fast) or Stopwatch (5, freezes non-boss enemies and their bullets for 2.5 s; bosses slow to 0.7×). A2 'Whip Spin' (hold up to 1.2 s): 90 r spin, 6 hits/s at 45%, move −40%, destroys arrows and bones it touches. Passive 'Blessed Lineage': HOLY cards +60% offer weight, and the cross/axe/holyWater hooks count +1 stack. Ult 'Grand Cross': six crosses rise for 0.6 s (invulnerable), then rocket outward with wall-pass and pierce, about 900% base total.
  - *Implementation:* kits/hunter.ts. Add a segment-vs-circle helper in core/math.ts for the capsule query (one spatial.query per swing). Reuse PROJ.CROSS, AXE, FLASK and DAGGER. Stopwatch sets w.freezeT: enemyAi multiplies dt by 0 for non-bosses and 0.7 for bosses, and enemy-team projectiles in updateProjectiles skip their step. Add pickups PICK.SMALLHEART (pink, ammo) and PICK.SUBWEAPON (one guaranteed per floor from STREAM.loot). The art (art/heroes/hunter.ts) is a chibi in a leather coat and red headband with big earnest eyes. The whip is a 10-segment chain extended by an ease on swingT (render-only).
  - *Acceptance:* The whip hits an enemy 149 u ahead but not one 40 u behind. Over 100 hits, tip hits average ≥ 1.45× non-tip hits. Stopwatch holds 140 enemies frozen for 300 ± 1 ticks while a boss keeps moving at 0.7×. Hearts never exceed 99. The capsule query costs < 0.02 ms per swing.
- **Dusk Dhampir (Duelist, motif: Crescent)** — Stats: HP 95, ARM 5, SPD 240, CRIT 10%. Uses 'Blood' (0–100, starts at 30, no passive regen; +3 per hit, +8 per kill). Primary 'Crimson Rapier': a 15/15/30 thrust combo every 0.28 s in a 95 u, 30° cone; the third thrust lunges 36 u and pierces. A1 'Mist Form' (25 blood): 1.0 s untargetable at +45% speed, passing through enemies (not walls) and applying Bleed (3% max HP/s for 4 s, 60 dps cap on non-bosses). A2 'Blood Pact': pay 15% current HP as true damage (can't kill) for +50 blood and +30% damage for 6 s. Passive 'Half-Blood': heal 1.5% max HP per kill and +5% lifesteal below 30% HP; BLOOD cards +60% weight, HOLY −50%. Ult 'Crimson Moon': 6 s of blood-red tint and +12% lifesteal; every kill fires 3 homing player-team BLOOD bolts for 90%.
  - *Implementation:* kits/dhampir.ts. Add EnemyStore.bleedDps and bleedT (Float32Array), ticked next to burn in world.ts. While mist is active, kit state sets a flag that hurtPlayer and enemy contact checks honour (p.invuln plus a no-push flag). The art is a pale chibi with a silver bob, big cartoon eyes with ruby irises (not monster glowEyes), tiny fangs, and a black coat with red lining echoing Count Vlador's cape.
  - *Acceptance:* Mist through a line of 5 skeletons applies 5 bleeds and takes 0 contact damage. Blood Pact at 3 HP leaves 1 HP. Blood persists across rooms via kitState.
- **Brass Alchemist (Artificer, motif: Hexagram)** — Stats: HP 80, ARM 3, SPD 215, EN 120 (+10/s). Primary 'Reagent Flask': a lob to the aim point (max 300 u) with a 55 r splash for 20 damage, in the current element. A1 'Transmute' (free, 0.4 s cooldown) cycles Ignis (burn 6 dps for 3 s) → Glacies (slow 40% for 2 s) → Fulgur (arcs to 2 foes for 50%). A2 'Homunculus Cauldron' (45 EN, 12 s): a waddling turret for 8 s that lobs flasks every 0.9 s. The flask reuses PF_LOB and PROJ.FLASK tinted per element. Reaction cooldowns are tracked per enemy in an aux Float32Array. The cauldron is a companion def (Epic 2 store). Statue gems come from STREAM.loot. The art: goggles on a mop of orange hair, a brass backpack with a bubbling green bottle, a leather apron.

#### BLD-5 · E5 · The Grimoire Expands: Card System v2 and an 80-Card Pool

*Combat systems & buildcraft · effort XL · impact 5/5*

Tiered cards with caps and prerequisites, TFT-style (Teamfight Tactics) tag resonance bonuses, offer tools (reroll, banish, skip, pity), floor-scaled rarity, 53 new cards across nine archetypes plus class cards, and a headless balance harness that gates it all. **Player value:** Every run can commit to a real build identity (bleed vampire, holy crusader, frost executioner, plague doctor, necromancer pet-tamer) with clear goals like 'two more BLOOD for resonance 6', and enough control over offers to chase them.

- **Card data v2: tiers, caps, prerequisites** — Cards define explicit values for tiers I, II and III instead of stacking linearly. maxStacks caps them: stat commons at 5, behaviour cards at 3, keystones at 1. Prerequisites gate some cards (Crimson Torrent only appears after a BLOOD card), and class cards are locked to their class. Maxed cards leave the pool. The level-up card shows a 'TIER II → III' diff.
  - *Implementation:* CardDef in cards.ts gains: tiers [TierSpec, TierSpec?, TierSpec?] (mods plus effect params), maxStacks, requires? {tag?, card?}, classes?: ClassId[], effect?: EffectId, and applies? {st, chance, stacks}. Player.recompute folds the tier spec at tier = min(stacks, maxStacks). The existing 27 cards are re-expressed so tier I equals today's values; fork is capped at 3 (6 shards). PlayerSave stays [id, stacks] and is clamped on load.
  - *Acceptance:* Schema test: unique ids, known tags, every icon exists in ui/icons.ts, tiers monotonic, maxStacks ≥ number of tiers. Fuzzing 10k offers never yields a maxed or prerequisite-locked card. Tier-I parity: with all 27 cards at 1 stack, sim damage matches pre-v2 within 1%.
- **Tag resonance thresholds** — Tag counts (already computed in Player.recompute but only used for ORBIT) unlock set bonuses at 3, 6 and 9 across 12 tags: the existing ARCANE, KINETIC, CRIT, ORBIT, HOLY, BLOOD and FLAME, plus new FROST, STORM, VENOM, SUMMON and HEX. Examples:
- HOLY: 3 = +1 heart per room clear; 6 = +25% HOLY and Exorcism radius ×2; 9 = every Item Crash also casts Requiem.
- BLOOD: 3 = bleed ticks heal 1%; 6 = crits add +1 bleed; 9 = overheal becomes a shield worth up to 30% max HP.
- FLAME: 3 = +2 max burn stacks; 6 = burning enemies explode on death for 40%; 9 = Witchfire spreads.
- FROST: 3 = hits on chilled enemies add +1 chill; 6 = frozen enemies take +60% PHYS; 9 = bosses can be frozen for 0.5 s.
- CRIT: 3 = +5% crit; 6 = overcrit converts at 1:1.5; 9 = the first hit on a full-HP enemy always crits.
